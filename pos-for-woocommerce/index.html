Why Webkul WooCommerce POS is the complete omnichannel toolkit

Bridge the gap between your physical outlets and your WooCommerce online store with one synced point of sale.

Real Time Inventory Sync
Stock levels update instantly across every outlet and your online store after each sale, so shelves, registers, and reports always agree on what remains.
Offline Selling Support
Keep billing customers when the internet drops. Orders are stored safely on the register and sync back to WooCommerce the moment the connection returns.
Outlet and Cashier Management
Create separate physical outlets, assign cashiers with their own logins, and track the sales performance of every store location individually.
Complete Hardware Compatibility
Connect barcode scanners, thermal receipt printers, card readers, and cash drawers out of the box, with no extra drivers or custom builds required.
Split Payment Checkout
Accept cash, card, and custom payment methods inside a single checkout session, letting customers divide one bill across several payment options.
Payment Terminal Integrations
Connect Stripe, Square, PayPal, and Linkly terminals so the order total reaches the card machine automatically and every transaction syncs into WooCommerce.
WhatsApp Order Notifications
Send order confirmations and invoice details straight to a customer's WhatsApp number, automatically or on demand, right from the register screen.
Desktop and Mobile Apps
Install the register as a Progressive Web App on desktops, tablets, and phones for a fast, native feeling checkout experience at any counter.
Direct Printing
Print receipts, drawer reports, barcode labels and PDFs straight to thermal, ZPL label and office printers without the browser print dialog, with a printer per outlet.
Z Report Shift Auditing
Close each register shift with a printable Z Report that reconciles expected cash against counted cash and archives every session for accounting.

POS Help Center
Find setup guides, cashier how-tos, troubleshooting fixes, an error message index, hardware compatibility, and support contacts, organized by role and by task.

Built for Every Store Type

Whether you run a single counter or a multi-location chain, Webkul WooCommerce Point of Sale adapts to your specific retail workflow:

  • Grocery & Supermarkets — Offline selling keeps checkout moving even when internet drops; fast barcode scanning by Product ID or SKU; and cash drawer shift auditing with printable 80mm thermal Z-Reports.
  • Fashion & Apparel Boutiques — Variation layouts (Card, Table, and List views) to pick sizes; direct barcode label printing; and checkout discounts and coupons applied directly beside the payment keypad.
  • Multi-Outlet Retail Chains — Unlimited physical outlets managed from one WooCommerce backend; centralized or master stock sync across all locations; bulk product assignment; and individual cashier permissions per counter.
  • Cafés & Quick-Service Food — Hold cart to pause orders during rushes and serve the next customer; split payment checkout across cash and cards; and custom checkout fees for service charges.
  • Electronics & Tech Retail — Payment terminal integrations (Stripe, Square, Linkly) that send order totals directly to the card reader without manual typing; and customizable invoice templates with business logo and tax numbers.
  • Wholesale & Trade Counters — Per-outlet pricing for location-specific trade rates; keyboard-driven bulk spreadsheet editing; and real-time inventory tracking against your online WooCommerce catalog.

What's New in WooCommerce POS 7.3.0

  • Direct Printing — send receipts and drawer reports straight to the thermal printer, barcode labels to a ZPL label printer and media-library PDFs to an A4 or laser printer, through a print service, with the browser print dialog as fallback
  • Thermal Printer per Outlet — give each outlet its own receipt printer, and let cashiers pick a printer for their session from the POS header
  • Invoice Template Fonts — set a font on any field of an invoice template, or one font for the whole invoice from the new Default Settings panel
  • Newest Cart Item First — an Appearance setting that shows the last scanned or added item at the top of the cart
  • Reliability and security — the POS drawer table is now created on MySQL 5.7, multisite admin screens load faster, and the setup wizard requires a logged-in POS manager

Recent Release Highlights

  • Bulk Assignment — set per-outlet stock, price and status for every product and variation from one spreadsheet screen, with search, keyboard editing, copy and paste from Excel or Google Sheets, and a single bulk save

  • Per-Outlet Pricing — give each outlet its own selling price for a product; the register shows it, charges it, and records it on the order

  • Custom Per-Outlet Columns — add your own Text, Long text or Number columns to the Bulk Assignment sheet for shelf locations, supplier references or per-outlet notes

  • Out of Stock Products at the Register — show items that have run out with an Out of stock badge instead of hiding them; they still can't be sold

  • Out-of-Stock Stock Management — the WooCommerce "Hide out of stock items from the catalog" setting no longer hides products and variations from POS stock screens, so an item that has run out can be restocked

  • Expanded Cart Submit Button Control — enable a Submit button on expanded cart items so quantity and custom price edits are confirmed in one deliberate tap

  • Percentage or Fixed Checkout Fees — charge a named custom fee such as a card surcharge or service charge directly from the checkout page

  • Collapsible Cart Totals — fold the totals breakdown into a compact Grand Total bar, with the register default set from the admin Appearance settings

  • Coupon Restrictions at the Register — minimum spend, product, and usage rules from WooCommerce now validate every coupon applied at the POS end

  • Checkout Discount & Coupon Management — apply percentage or fixed discounts and redeem coupons on the checkout page, right beside the payment keypad

  • Improved Drawer Reports — redesigned Print Drawer Reports history in the POS theme with date filtering and drawer reconciliation fixes

  • Z-Report Generation & Printing — reconcile daily sales, verify cash drawer balances, and print end of shift audit reports on thermal receipt printers

  • Notification Tones — optional sound feedback for product taps, keypad presses and drawer close, switched on for the store or per outlet

  • Multiple Variation Layouts — Card View, Table View, or List View layout modes to speed up variable product selection at the register

  • Stock Visibility Controls — show or hide live stock levels in the POS product grid with a single toggle in the WordPress admin panel

  • Multi-Pricing, Filters & Product Pinning — multi-price products, attribute-based catalog filters with On Sale and Featured badges, and pin visibility options for frequent sellers
