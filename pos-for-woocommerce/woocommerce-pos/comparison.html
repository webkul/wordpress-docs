WooCommerce POS Plugin Comparison

Choosing the right point of sale for a WooCommerce store is a long-term decision: it shapes how your outlets sell, how inventory stays accurate, and how much you pay over the years. This WooCommerce POS plugin comparison puts Webkul WooCommerce Point of Sale side by side with four widely used alternatives — wePOS, Openpos, FooSales, and YITH Point of Sale — so you can judge each solution on the capabilities that matter in a real store.

Webkul WooCommerce POS is a native WooCommerce plugin with a React-powered register, offline selling with automatic sync, multi-outlet and cashier management, centralized or master stock inventory, and a large family of add-ons covering payment terminals, kiosks, restaurants, delivery, and more.

How this comparison was prepared

Competitor capabilities below are taken from each vendor's own public website and documentation as of August 2026. Where a vendor does not document a capability, the table says Not documented rather than assuming it is missing. Features and prices change — always verify current details with the vendor before purchase.

Key Advantages of Webkul WooCommerce Point of Sale

Offline selling is built in, not an afterthought. The register keeps billing when the internet drops and syncs every order back to WooCommerce automatically once the connection returns. Among the compared plugins, one vendor explicitly documents that offline mode is not included, and another does not document it at all.

Real multi-outlet retail. Create unlimited physical outlets, assign products, stock, and cashiers per outlet, and choose between centralized stock or master stock distribution — a model designed for chains, not just a single counter.

Complete cash discipline. Every shift runs through drawer opening balances, a live transaction ledger, counted-cash reconciliation, and a printable Z-Report for end-of-day auditing on 80mm thermal printers.

Payment terminals without manual re-typing. Dedicated add-ons connect Stripe, Square, PayPal, Linkly, and Stripe M2 terminals so the order total reaches the card machine automatically — several compared plugins either rely on standard web gateways or document no terminal connection.

A genuine product ecosystem. More than 25 add-ons extend the same register into kiosk self-checkout, restaurant kitchens, delivery management, gift cards, loyalty programs, warehouse management, and AI reporting — you grow the system instead of replacing it.

Predictable cost. Webkul POS is sold as a one-time purchase license rather than a monthly platform fee, which typically matters most for multi-register stores where per-seat subscriptions accumulate.

Where Competing Solutions Fall Short

The points below use each vendor's own public statements; they are limitations to weigh, not criticisms of the products.

  • wePOS documents unlimited outlets and a fast REST-API register, but its site does not document offline selling, payment terminal integrations, or detailed hardware support — verify these before purchase if they matter to your store.
  • Openpos covers offline mode and broad hardware well; it is sold under a CodeCanyon license where included support is time-limited, and its terminal payments run through standard WooCommerce gateways rather than dedicated terminal connectors.
  • FooSales offers polished iPad and Android apps with offline mode, but it is subscription based, and multi-outlet management is not documented on its features page — confirm both against your outlet count and budget.
  • YITH Point of Sale states in its own FAQ that offline mode is not included, that no automatic payment gateway connects to the POS, and that receipts are courtesy documents rather than tax receipts; barcode generation requires an additional YITH extension.

Which Businesses Should Choose Webkul POS

  • Multi-outlet retail chains that need per-outlet stock, staff, and reporting under one WooCommerce store.
  • Grocery and high-volume counters that depend on barcode speed, offline resilience, and cash drawer discipline.
  • Restaurants and cafés using the kitchen display and delivery add-ons on the same register.
  • Omnichannel sellers who want online and in-store orders, refunds, and inventory in one WooCommerce dashboard.
  • Growing businesses that prefer a one-time license and an add-on path over per-month, per-register subscription fees.
