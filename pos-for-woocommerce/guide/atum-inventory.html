ATUM Inventory Management

Introduction

Version 7.4.0 makes WooCommerce POS work with ATUM Inventory Management for WooCommerce. If your store already uses ATUM to manage WooCommerce stock, the POS now fits in with it rather than working against it.

There is nothing to switch on. The compatibility is active only while ATUM is installed and active, and only with WooCommerce stock management turned on under WooCommerce → Settings → Products → Inventory (ATUM itself needs this). Stores without ATUM behave exactly as before.

What changes depends on the Select Inventory Type chosen under Point of Sale → Settings → General → POS Panel Configurations:

Inventory typeWhat the POS does with ATUM active
Master Stock InventoryPOS orders sell the outlet's own POS stock and leave WooCommerce stock, which ATUM manages, untouched.
Centralized Stock InventoryThe register sells WooCommerce stock, respects ATUM's out-of-stock threshold, and checks stock again before an online POS order is created.

Master Stock Inventory

With master stock, every outlet sells from its own POS stock. With ATUM active:

  • A POS order reduces only the outlet's POS stock. It no longer changes WooCommerce stock as well, so the same sale is never taken off WooCommerce stock twice.
  • Completing, cancelling or refunding a POS order, or editing its line items in the admin, does not raise or lower WooCommerce stock.
  • WooCommerce stock stays under ATUM's control, so ATUM's stock counts and reports stay accurate.

Manage outlet stock as usual

Outlet stock is still set from Products & Inventory and the Bulk Assignment sheet. ATUM does not change it.

Centralized Stock Inventory

With centralized inventory, the register and your online store share WooCommerce stock. With ATUM active, two things change.

ATUM's Out-of-Stock Threshold at the Register

ATUM can treat a product as out of stock while a few units are still on the shelf, using its Out of Stock Threshold setting. When that setting is on in ATUM, the register follows the same rule:

  • The stock shown at the register is the WooCommerce quantity minus the product's threshold. For example, with 10 in stock and a threshold of 3, the register shows and sells 7.
  • A product at or below its threshold is treated as out of stock at the register. It follows the Show Out of Stock Products setting and cannot be sold.
  • When the cashier clicks Pay, lines that would take a product below its threshold are flagged in the stock check, as with any other item that has run out.

Products that allow backorders, or have no threshold, are not affected. Variations that share their parent's stock are checked together against the parent's quantity.

Stock Check Before an Online Order Is Created

The register loads its stock when it starts, so a website sale made in the meantime can use up stock that the register still shows. With ATUM active, an online POS order is now checked against current WooCommerce stock, less any ATUM threshold, before it is created.

If the stock no longer covers the cart, the order is not created and the register shows:

Not enough stock for: Product name (available: N). Please update the cart and try again.

Reduce the quantity or remove the listed products, then take payment again. The cart and the cashier's session are kept.

Offline orders are not rejected

This check applies only to orders placed while the register is online. Centralized inventory does not take orders offline, so orders already placed are never rejected when they sync.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to change any POS setting for ATUM? No. The compatibility turns on by itself while ATUM is active, and turns off when ATUM is deactivated.

Why does the register show less stock than WooCommerce? The product has an ATUM out-of-stock threshold and your inventory type is centralized. The register shows only the stock above the threshold.

Will POS sales still appear in ATUM? POS orders are normal WooCommerce orders. With centralized inventory they reduce WooCommerce stock as usual. With master stock they reduce only the outlet's POS stock.
