Configuring Happy Hours Settings

After activating the plugin, store administrators can customize the customer-facing and cashier-facing text strings that appear on the POS terminal countdown timer, cart discounts, and receipts.

Accessing Configuration Settings

  1. From the WordPress administrative sidebar, navigate to Point of Sale → Settings.
  2. Select the Happy Hours tab (indicated by an hourglass icon).

Configuration Fields

The settings page includes the following customizable fields:

FieldDescriptionDefault ValueWhere It Appears in POS
Countdown Timer TextThe label shown on the interactive countdown timer widget in the POS header.Happy HoursTop-right header bar on the POS home and category screens during active promotional windows.
Happy Hour TitleThe title identifying promotional discounts applied at the product or category level.Happy HourPrinted on POS order receipts and invoice summaries next to qualifying discounted items.
Happy Hours Discount Fee TitleThe title assigned to overall basket and cart-level promotional savings.Happy Hours DiscountThe cart total summary row, payment modal breakdown, and printed invoice receipt.
Happy Hour Discount TextThe descriptive label displayed next to individual discounted product amounts.Happy Hour DiscountItem line rows inside the POS active cart view (e.g. -$2.70 Discount).

Step-by-Step Configuration

  1. In the Countdown Timer Text field, enter the heading you want cashiers and counter shoppers to see on the live clock (e.g., Flash Deal or Happy Hours).
  2. In the Happy Hour Title field, enter your preferred receipt label for itemized product promotions.
  3. In the Happy Hours Discount Fee Title field, specify the label for cart total discounts.
  4. In the Happy Hour Discount Text field, provide the tag for line-item cart deductions.
  5. Click the blue Save Configurations button at the bottom of the panel.

Once saved, WordPress updates the plugin options, and the updated labels are transmitted to all connected POS registers upon their next session load or browser refresh.
