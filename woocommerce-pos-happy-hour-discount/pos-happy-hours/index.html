Introduction to WooCommerce POS Happy Hours Discount

WooCommerce POS Happy Hours Discount enables store owners to schedule automated promotional pricing windows at their physical retail checkout counters. By setting specific dates, hours, and discount parameters, retailers can drive foot traffic during off-peak times, reward registered shoppers, and boost inventory turnover.

│  - Define Happy Hour schedules (dates, start & end)    │
│  - Set Product, Category, or Cart Total discounts      │
│  - Target All Customers or selected VIP shoppers       │

Purpose-Built for Retail Outlets

Unlike online coupon systems where shoppers must enter alphanumeric codes, Happy Hours discounts apply automatically at the point of sale during active promotional periods, speeding up counter checkout and eliminating cashier calculation errors.

Key Benefits for Physical Store Owners

  • Off-Peak Traffic Generation: Attract afternoon or weekday shoppers with time-limited discounts on select products or menus.
  • Automated Price Adjustments: Cashiers do not need to memorize sale percentages or manually apply cart fees—the system calculates exact savings in real time.
  • Granular Campaign Control: Target individual products, whole product categories, or overall basket totals with fixed or percentage deductions.
  • VIP & Loyalty Alignment: Restrict special happy hour rates to specific registered customer accounts or make them available to all walk-in shoppers.
  • Transparent Customer Invoices: Printed thermal receipts and screen invoices explicitly list the Happy Hour discount amount, building customer trust and loyalty.
