Export Products & Edit eBay Listing Workspace

The Export Products tab (WC Ebay Connector → Manage Account → Export Products) allows you to publish simple and variable WooCommerce catalog items to your connected eBay marketplace account.

1. Catalog Export Table

The export table lists all eligible products in your WooCommerce catalog:

  • Selection Checkbox: Select individual items or use the header checkbox to select all visible rows.
  • Image & Name: Product thumbnail and name.
  • SKU: Product SKU (mandatory for accurate variation tracking).
  • Stock & Regular Price: Catalog stock availability and base retail price.
  • Categories: Assigned WooCommerce categories.
  • Export Status: Indicates whether the product is Not Exported, Exported, or Pending Update.

2. Export Actions

Export Selected Products

  1. Select the desired items using the checkboxes.
  2. Select Export to eBay from the bulk actions dropdown and click Apply.
  3. The connector validates categories, item specifics, and price rules, then sends the payload to eBay's AddItem or AddFixedPriceItem API endpoint.

Export All

Click the Export All button to queue the entire eligible WooCommerce catalog for export. Products configured in the Exclude Products Registry are automatically filtered out.

Native WooCommerce "Products > All Products" Bulk Export

In addition to the connector's internal export table, you can export or revise products directly from WordPress's native product catalog list (Products → All Products):

  1. Navigate to Products → All Products in the WordPress admin.
  2. Select the products you wish to export using the row checkboxes.
  3. Open the standard Bulk actions dropdown above or below the product list.
  4. Select Export/Update on eBay (Your Account Name) and click Apply.
  5. Selected products are queued in the background (FM_EBAY_Export_Product_Queue), with an administrative progress notice displaying real-time completion status and queue links.

3. The Edit eBay Listing Workspace

In version 5.0.0, merchants can fine-tune listing details on a per-product basis before exporting to eBay. Click the Edit Listing action on any product row in the Export Products table to open the dedicated workspace (action=edit-listing&product_id=N).

The workspace is organized into focused sub-tabs:

General Settings

Configure core listing metadata, condition, custom pricing, and item identifiers.

  • Listing Title & Subtitle: Override the default WooCommerce product title with an eBay-optimized title (up to 80 characters) and optional promotional subtitle (up to 55 characters).
  • Custom SKU & Identifiers: Set a dedicated eBay SKU or barcode identifier (UPC, EAN, ISBN) without modifying the store's WooCommerce catalog item.
  • Listing Duration: Set listing lifecycle (Days_30 or GTC - Good 'Til Cancelled).
  • Item Condition: Select the exact eBay condition (New, New with tags, New without tags, Refurbished, Used - Excellent, etc.) and provide an item condition description.
  • Price & Three-Tier Inventory Modes: Set custom pricing or choose from three distinct inventory synchronization behaviors:
    • Store Stock (woo): Mirrors live WooCommerce stock directly. Any sale in WooCommerce or on eBay decrements store inventory.
    • Custom Fixed (custom): Sets an independent, static quantity on eBay without reading or writing WooCommerce stock (ideal for made-to-order goods, dropshipping, or print-on-demand).
    • Reserved Allocation Batch (allocation): Hands a dedicated batch of units to eBay out of your shop stock. The batch can never exceed warehouse stock, and if in-store sales drop your physical shelf count below the allocation, the connector automatically clamps the eBay batch down so you never oversell.

Item Specifics

Tailor category-required and recommended item specifics to maximize eBay search visibility and filter match rates.

  • Category Attributes: Automatically pulls required and recommended specifics from eBay's taxonomy for the mapped primary category (e.g., Brand, MPN, Model, Product Type, HS Code).
  • Custom Item Specifics: Click Add Specific to define custom attribute-value pairs required by niche categories or buyer search habits.
  • Validation: Enforces eBay attribute limits and prevents invalid formatting before submitting to the API.

Shipping & Return Policies

Override account-level default business policies for individual products that have unique handling requirements.

  • Shipping Policy Override: Select a custom shipping profile or configure dedicated domestic and international shipping services, handling dispatch times, and postal code origins.
  • Return Policy Override: Customize return acceptance (Returns Accepted or Returns Not Accepted), return window (14 Days, 30 Days, 60 Days), refund method, and return shipping payer (Buyer or Seller / Free Returns).
  • Package Dimensions & Weight: Override package weight and dimensions specifically for eBay calculated shipping rates without altering WooCommerce shipping classes.

Template Studio

Assign and preview custom listing templates created in the Visual Listing Template Builder.

  • Template Assignment: Choose whether this product uses the account default template or a dedicated listing design selected from your Visual Listing Template Builder library.
  • Live Preview: Review the rendered description with real product attributes and images populated before committing to eBay.

4. Native WooCommerce Product Editor Meta Box

For day-to-day catalog maintenance, merchants do not need to navigate into the connector's menus to review or adjust basic listing parameters for individual products.

The connector registers an eBay Details meta box directly on the standard WooCommerce single product editor (post.php?post=N&action=edit):

  • eBay Condition: Choose the eBay item condition (New, Used, Refurbished, etc.) directly from a dropdown on the product page.
  • Mapped Categories Overview: Displays the primary and storefront eBay categories currently mapped to this product's WooCommerce category.
  • Condition Description: Enter custom condition notes visible to eBay buyers.
  • eBay Product ID Reference: Displays the active eBay Item ID if the item is already exported, confirming synchronization.
  • Shipping & Return Snapshot: Displays the assigned shipping service, calculated/flat pricing type, and return acceptance terms currently governing the item.

5. Pre-Export Verification & Resolving Common Errors

Before exporting catalog products to eBay, ensure your product data complies with eBay listing standards:

Pre-Export Checklist

  • Inventory & Stock: Ensure the product has managed stock with quantity greater than zero (or a default quantity configured in settings).
  • Item Condition: Select an appropriate condition (New, Used, etc.) either in the product meta box or in the Edit Listing workspace.
  • Product Images: Upload high-resolution product photography (minimum 500px, preferably 1000px+ for eBay zoom).
  • Mapped Category: Verify that the product's assigned WooCommerce category is mapped to an active eBay leaf category.

Resolving Error: "The product category is not mapped with the eBay category"

If an administrative error notice appears stating that the category is unmapped:

  1. Navigate to WC Ebay Connector → Manage Account → Category Mapping.
  2. Click Map Category and map your store category to the target eBay leaf node.
  3. Once saved, return to the product edit page and confirm the product is assigned to the mapped category.
  4. Retry the export action.

Resolving Missing Attribute / Item Specifics Errors

If mandatory eBay specifications are missing, eBay will reject the listing payload with an attribute validation error:

  1. Open the WooCommerce product editor and navigate to Product Data → Attributes (or use the Item Specifics tab in the Edit eBay Listing workspace).
  2. Enter values for all required attributes (such as Brand, MPN, Size, or Model).
  3. Save the attributes and update the product.

  1. Once the mandatory attributes are populated, re-export the item to eBay.

6. Verifying Live Listings on eBay

Once products are exported successfully, the connector updates their status to Exported and records the 12-digit eBay Item ID.

You can verify published items directly in your eBay Seller Hub under Listings → Active:

  • Review the live listing title, image gallery, condition description, and price.
  • Confirm that your visual description template renders cleanly on desktop and mobile browsers.
