Features & Capabilities

WooCommerce eBay Connector provides a complete integration suite for WordPress merchants looking to expand their sales across international eBay marketplaces.

1. Store Onboarding & Account Management

  • Interactive Setup Wizard: Guided 5-step onboarding that verifies eBay API keys, validates user credentials, selects marketplace sites, and configures default categories in real time.
  • Multi-Account Hub: Connect and manage multiple eBay accounts (across different eBay sites like US, UK, Australia, Germany) from a single WordPress dashboard.
  • Account Status Monitoring: Live health indicators (Connected / Disconnected) with one-click API limit check buttons.
  • Credential Protection: Retains saved authentication tokens securely when editing accounts, preventing accidental credential overwrites.

2. Catalog & Listing Management

  • Visual Listing Template Builder: Drag-and-drop template designer for creating professional, responsive eBay product descriptions with section palettes, typography styling, and mobile responsive views.
  • Template Starter Layouts: Pre-designed layout starters (Minimal, Modern, Bold, Showcase) for rapid template deployment.
  • eBay Policy Compliance Check: Built-in verification bar that scans templates against eBay's active-content policies (detecting disallowed scripts, target attributes, and non-HTTPS assets).
  • Edit eBay Listing Workspace: Product-level editing interface to adjust eBay titles, subtitles, duration, condition descriptions, variation photo sets, and shipping policies independently of WooCommerce product data.
  • Category Item Specifics & Custom Attributes: Automatically imports required and recommended eBay aspects as WooCommerce attributes upon category mapping, with per-product custom item specifics in the Edit Listing workspace.
  • Bidirectional Product Sync: Import simple and variable products from eBay to WooCommerce, or export WooCommerce catalog products to eBay.
  • Native WooCommerce Bulk Export: Export or update products on eBay directly from the native WordPress Products → All Products bulk actions dropdown.
  • Three-Tier Inventory Modes: Choose between live Store Stock mirroring (woo), independent Custom Fixed quantities (custom), or a Reserved Allocation Batch (allocation) that auto-clamps to prevent overselling.

3. Background Processing & Rate-Limit Resilience

  • Background Queues: High-capacity export, update, and import jobs executed through Action Scheduler with live progress indicators and error logging.
  • Rate-Limit Auto-Pause & Resume: Automatically pauses product import jobs when approaching eBay API call thresholds and resumes them when quota resets.
  • Queue Controls: Retry failed tasks, stop running queues, or resume paused jobs on demand.
  • Background Image Queue: Separate background queue for importing product images to prevent PHP execution timeouts during large batch imports.

4. Order Management & Stock Control

  • Automated Order Import: Scheduled cron imports paid eBay orders into WooCommerce every 15 minutes, with duplicate order checks.
  • Manual Order Import: Fetch orders on demand by date range or specific eBay Order ID.
  • Auto Create Missing Order Products: Automatically generates new WooCommerce products for items sold on eBay if they do not yet exist in the store catalog.
  • Inventory Synchronization: Decrements stock in WooCommerce upon eBay order import, and updates eBay stock levels when orders are placed on WooCommerce.
  • Email Suppression: Automatically disables WooCommerce buyer transactional emails for imported eBay orders to avoid duplicate customer notifications.
  • HPOS Compatible with Order Source Column: Compatible with WooCommerce High-Performance Order Storage (Custom Order Tables), with a native Order Source column on the main WooCommerce Orders list.

5. Pricing, Shipping & Policies

  • Flexible Price Rules: Configure percentage or fixed markups/markdowns on imported and exported products to account for marketplace fees and currency differences.
  • Shipping Policy Mapping: Support for Flat, Calculated, and Freight shipping policies, including handling time, free shipping, and local pickup.
  • Return Policy Controls: Define return eligibility (14, 30, or 60 days), refund methods (Money Back, Replacement), and return shipping fee responsibility (Buyer / Seller).
  • Selective Sync Exclusions: Blacklist specific WooCommerce products or eBay item IDs to prevent them from syncing between WooCommerce and eBay.
  • Duplicate SKU Guard: Option to prevent duplicate product creation when an imported item's SKU already exists in WooCommerce.

6. Supported Global eBay Marketplaces

WooCommerce eBay Connector supports official eBay marketplace sites worldwide, allowing multi-account merchants to manage localized catalogs and currencies:

  • North America: eBay United States (US), eBay Canada (English & French - CA, CAFR), eBay Motors (Motors).
  • United Kingdom & Ireland: eBay United Kingdom (UK), eBay Ireland (IE).
  • Europe: Germany (DE), France (FR), Italy (IT), Spain (ES), Austria (AT), Switzerland (CH), Netherlands (NL), Belgium (Dutch & French - NLBE, FRBE), Poland (PL).
  • Asia-Pacific & Australia: Australia (AU), India (IN), Hong Kong (HK), Malaysia (MY), Philippines (PH), Singapore (SG).

eBay Motors & Vehicle Fitment Compatibility

For automotive parts manufacturers, retailers, and aftermarket suppliers, the connector integrates seamlessly with the companion WooCommerce eBay Item Compatibility extension:

  • Master Vehicle List (MVL) & Parts Compatibility: Map automotive components to year, make, model, trim, and engine variants.
  • Enhanced Buyer Search: Products appear in eBay's parts finder tool ("Find parts for your vehicle"), boosting sales conversion and eliminating part return disputes.
  • Bidirectional Fitment Sync: Import existing vehicle fitment listings from eBay Motors into WooCommerce or export parts compatibility tables from WooCommerce to eBay Motors.
