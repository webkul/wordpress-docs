Features

WooCommerce Happy Hours Discount provides a comprehensive suite of promotional tools tailored for WooCommerce store owners. Below is a detailed breakdown of all administrative and storefront capabilities.

Administrative Capabilities

1. Flexible Promotional Scheduling

  • Configure exact Start Date and End Date using intuitive calendar pickers.
  • Define precise Start Time and End Time down to the minute.
  • Prevent scheduling errors with built-in validation that checks for past dates and conflicting overlapping promotional timeframes.

2. Multi-Tiered Discount Scope

Create complex discount rules by combining one or more discount criteria within a single promotional rule:

  • Product-Specific Discounts: Target one or more individual products from your catalog.
  • Category-Wide Discounts: Apply discounts automatically to all products categorized under selected store departments.
  • Cart Total Thresholds: Grant discounts when a shopper's cart subtotal reaches a specified minimum and maximum spend range.

3. Dual Calculation Modes

Choose between two distinct calculation methodologies for each discount block:

  • Fixed Amount Discount: Deduct an exact fixed amount (e.g., $7.00 off per item or order).
  • Percentage Discount: Calculate a percentage deduction (e.g., 15% off regular price or cart subtotal).

4. Audience Targeting & Segmentation

Control who qualifies for promotional pricing:

  • All Customers: Available to every storefront visitor, including guest shoppers.
  • Select Customer(s): Search and designate specific registered customer accounts by user ID, customer name, or email address.

5. Structured Rule Management

  • Comprehensive list view displaying Rule Name, Rule Type, Start/End Dates, Start/End Times, Rule Status, and Creation Date.
  • Instant search bar to filter rules by name.
  • Bulk actions to Enable, Disable, or Delete multiple rules simultaneously.
  • Quick hover action links to Edit or Delete individual promotional rules.

6. Full Appearance & Label Personalization

  • Countdown Timer Text: Personalize the title displayed inside the hanging signboard (default: "Happy Hours").
  • Discount Fee Title: Customize the negative fee line label displayed in the cart and checkout for cart-based discounts.
  • Discount Line Item Text: Set the itemized label that appears under qualifying product names.
  • Color Pickers: Customize container background color and typography color to match store branding.
  • Width Slider: Fine-tune the banner container width with a dynamic slider (0–50px increase).

Storefront & Customer Experience

7. Interactive Hanging Signboard Widget

  • Renders as an eye-catching hanging signboard complete with decorative hanging strings and pins.
  • Animated real-time countdown showing remaining Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds (e.g., 10d:08h:14m:12s).
  • Automatically updates every second on the client side without reloading the browser page.

8. Custom Shortcode Support

Deploy the countdown signboard anywhere across your WordPress site using the shortcode:

[wkwc_hhd_happy_hours_shortcode]

Use it in blog posts, landing pages, sidebar widgets, or custom block layouts.

9. Transparent Cart & Checkout Calculation

  • Product & Category Discounts: Reflected in real time on cart item data and order item lines.
  • Cart Total Discounts: Added as a clear, negative fee deduction under the cart subtotal.
  • Sales Tax Protection: Happy hour promotional fees are treated as tax-exempt negative fees, ensuring accurate tax calculations for store accountants.

10. Clear Customer Order Records

  • Customers receive order confirmation emails detailing itemized happy hour discounts.
  • View Order summaries in the My Account section display complete promotional breakdowns.
