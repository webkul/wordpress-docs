WooCommerce Happy Hours Discount

WooCommerce Happy Hours Discount empowers online merchants to run dynamic, time-sensitive promotional campaigns directly within their WooCommerce stores. By scheduling dedicated promotional windows ("Happy Hours"), store owners can stimulate sales during off-peak hours, clear seasonal inventory, and reward customer loyalty with automated discounts.

The extension introduces an engaging hanging signboard countdown timer on the storefront, keeping shoppers informed in real time about active promotions and creating compelling purchasing urgency.

Proven Flash Sales Strategy

Time-limited promotions and countdown timers have been proven to accelerate buyer decision-making and reduce checkout abandonment by creating transparent, deadline-driven incentives.

Key Benefits for Merchants

  • Automated Scheduling: Define precise start and end dates and times for each promotional campaign without needing manual intervention to toggle sale prices.
  • Multi-Level Discount Logic: Apply discounts at the individual Product level, across entire Product Categories, or based on overall Cart Total spending thresholds.
  • Targeted Audiences: Make happy hour sales available to all store visitors or restrict exclusive discounts to specific registered customers.
  • Urgency-Driving Storefront Timer: Automatically display a stylized hanging signboard countdown timer on the WooCommerce Shop page or anywhere on your website via a shortcode.
  • Flexible Discount Types: Configure either fixed dollar amount discounts or percentage-based savings for any rule.
  • Transparent Order Records: Itemized discounts and promotional fees are seamlessly recorded in cart lines, checkout totals, order confirmations, and customer account summaries.
  • HPOS & Block Compatibility: Built natively to integrate with WooCommerce High-Performance Order Storage (HPOS) and modern block-based Cart and Checkout experiences.
