FAQ & Notes

Review answers to commonly asked merchant questions, troubleshooting tips, and technical details for WooCommerce Happy Hours Discount.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Do Happy Hour discounts permanently change my catalog regular or sale prices?

No. Happy Hour discounts do not alter your database product prices. The extension dynamically calculates and applies promotional deductions during the active timeframe at the cart and checkout stages. Once the happy hour concludes, regular pricing immediately resumes.

2. How does the system handle timezones?

All Happy Hour start and end times follow your store's primary WordPress timezone setting configured under Settings → General → Timezone. Ensure your server and WordPress timezone match your commercial operating region.

3. What is the evaluation priority when multiple discount rules are active?

When an order qualifies for multiple discount tiers, the plugin evaluates them in the following automated priority order:

1Product DiscountHighest Priority
→
2Category DiscountSecondary Priority
→
3Cart Total DiscountOrder Level

Product Discount (Highest Priority)  →  Category Discount (Secondary Priority)  →  Cart Total Discount (Order Level)

  • Product Rule: Dedicated product rules evaluate first and take precedence over broader category rules for matching items.
  • Category Rule: If an item does not have a direct product discount, any active category rule applies next.
  • Cart Total Rule: Evaluates against the remaining cart subtotal and applies as an overall negative order fee.

4. Can I place the countdown timer on custom landing pages or blog posts?

Yes. Use the official shortcode anywhere shortcodes are accepted:

[wkwc_hhd_happy_hours_shortcode]

You can place it in WordPress block content, classic pages, Elementor or Divi templates, and text widget sidebars.

5. Is the plugin compatible with High-Performance Order Storage (HPOS)?

Yes. WooCommerce Happy Hours Discount declares full compatibility with WooCommerce Custom Order Tables (HPOS). Order line metadata and fee records are stored natively within wp_wc_orders and wp_woocommerce_order_itemmeta.

6. Does the extension work with WooCommerce Cart & Checkout Blocks?

Yes. The extension is compatible with modern block-based Cart and Checkout experiences as well as classic shortcode layouts ([woocommerce_cart] and [woocommerce_checkout]).

7. Can I run happy hour promotions exclusively for VIP or wholesale customers?

Yes. In the rule editor, change the Customers setting from All to Select Customer(s). You can then search and pick specific registered user accounts eligible for the promotion.

8. Is WordPress Multisite supported?

Yes. The extension incorporates native multisite network compatibility. When network-activated, database tables are deployed across all existing and newly created network subsites.

9. What occurs if a customer starts checkout right as Happy Hours end?

Promotional discounts are validated during cart updates and final order placement. If the scheduled timeframe expires before checkout submission, WooCommerce refreshes totals to standard rates to protect merchant margins.
