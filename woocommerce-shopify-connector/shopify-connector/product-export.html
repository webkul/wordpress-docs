Exporting Products

Export sends WooCommerce products to Shopify — manually in bulk, or automatically whenever a new product is published.

Open the store workspace and go to Products → Export Product.

What gets exported

Title, description, product type, tags, featured and gallery images (at the size chosen in Imported Image Size), attributes and variants, SKUs, prices and compare-at prices, weights, barcodes, and stock quantities. Inventory is written to the Shopify location selected in General Configuration (Select Shop Location). Simple and variable products are supported. If the product's category is mapped, the product is added to the mapped Shopify collection.

If Price Rule Applicable For is set to Export Product, matching price rules adjust prices on the way out.

Manual export

  1. The table lists your WooCommerce products with Store Product ID, Product Image, Product Name, Product Type, Product SKU, Sync Status, Export Status.
  2. Selective Export — Tick the products to send, choose Bulk actions → Export, and select Apply.
  3. Export All Products — Click the Export All Products button in the toolbar to submit all unexported WooCommerce products to the export queue in one step.
  4. Refresh Products — Click Refresh Products in the toolbar to re-sync all exported products in the background.

A progress panel ("Exporting Products") tracks the operation. Once finished, the Export Status column marks the products as exported. Products already on Shopify are skipped with "Product Already Exported".

Pushing later changes

After a product has been exported, edits made in WooCommerce are not sent automatically unless real-time sync is on. To push changes manually:

  • Tick the products and use Bulk actions → Update To Shopify.
  • Or click Refresh Products in the toolbar to update all previously mapped products. Products that were never exported are reported: "Products are not updated due to unexported on Shopify."

Item-Wise Shopify Listing Editor

The connector includes a dedicated listing editor allowing store managers to customize how an individual product appears and syncs on Shopify, without modifying WooCommerce core product data.

To access the editor, select Edit Shopify Listing on any product row in either the Export or Import product table.

The editor provides three tabbed configuration areas:

1. General Tab

  • Shopify Title Override — Specify a distinct product title for the Shopify storefront while preserving the WooCommerce product title.
  • Custom Price Rule / Adjustment — Apply a fixed or percentage price adjustment (+/-) specifically for this item on Shopify, overriding global price rules.
  • Stock Safety Buffer — Set a safety stock reserve (e.g. 5 units). The connector subtracts this buffer before syncing stock to Shopify, preventing overselling during traffic peaks.
  • Vendor & Product Type — Assign custom Shopify vendor and product type labels.
  • Tags Override — Specify comma-separated Shopify tags.
  • Field Sync Controls — Toggle sync permission on or off for individual attributes (Title, Description, Images, Price, Inventory, Barcode, Weight). Locked fields will not be overwritten by incoming or outgoing sync.

2. Variations Tab (Variable Products)

Configure per-variant overrides for variable products:

  • Individual price adjustments and stock safety buffers per variation.
  • Custom SKU and Barcode / GTIN overrides per variation.
  • Custom weight overrides per variation.

3. Shipping & Customs Tab

Configure international fulfillment and customs attributes required by Shopify:

  • Delivery Profile — Assign product to a specific Shopify shipping delivery profile.
  • Weight & Package Dimensions — Set exact fulfillment package specifications.
  • Country of Origin — ISO 2-letter country code for international trade compliance.
  • HS Tariff Code — Harmonized System tariff code for automated customs declarations.

Reset to Defaults

If you wish to remove item-specific overrides and revert back to global store settings, select Reset to Defaults at the bottom of the editor.

Bulk Export from WooCommerce Products List

You can export or update products to Shopify directly from the core WooCommerce product catalog table (Products → All Products), without opening the connector workspace.

  1. Navigate to Products → All Products (edit.php?post_type=product).
  2. Select the products you wish to export or update using the row checkboxes.
  3. In the Bulk actions dropdown, select Export/Update to Shopify (if you have multiple connected stores, the menu displays store-specific actions, e.g. Export/Update to Shopify (Store Name)).
  4. Click Apply.
  5. An interactive real-time progress queue modal opens:
    • Displays real-time progress percentage and item counter ("Running in the background: X of Y processed").
    • Item status badges track each product: Queued, Syncing, Exported, Updated, or Failed.
    • Action controls: Retry Failed to re-attempt errored items, or Stop Process to halt the queue.
    • The process runs asynchronously in the background — you can safely leave or refresh the page while it completes.

Single Product Edit — Shopify Connector Settings Metabox

When editing any product in WooCommerce (Products → Edit Product), the connector provides a native Shopify Connector Settings metabox for fast, item-level overrides:

Available controls:

  • Exclude this product from Shopify Sync & Auto-Export: Check this option to pause all outgoing exports, incoming updates, and stock synchronization for this specific item.
  • Shopify Title Override: Set a customized title for the Shopify storefront without changing the product name in WooCommerce.
  • Item Price Rule:
    • Use Global Store Default — Inherits global store settings and price rules.
    • Custom Fixed Price ($) — Overrides price with an exact fixed dollar value.
    • Increase by Percentage (+%) — Increases item price on Shopify by a specified percentage.
    • Decrease by Percentage (-%) — Decreases item price on Shopify by a specified percentage.
  • Safety Stock Buffer: Reserve a buffer quantity (e.g. 5 units). The connector subtracts this buffer before pushing inventory to Shopify, protecting you from overselling during high-volume spikes.

Automatic export of new products

To export every newly published WooCommerce product automatically to one or multiple Shopify stores:

  1. In the connector's left panel, under Product Rules, choose Export Product Rule — the Auto Export Product(s) Settings screen.
  2. Set Auto Product Creation to Enable.
  3. In Select Shopify Accounts, choose one or more connected Shopify stores to receive newly published products.
  4. Select Save Settings.

From then on, whenever a product is published in WooCommerce, it is automatically exported to all selected Shopify stores in the background.

Real-time updates

With Product Auto-Sync set to WooCommerce To Shopify or Both Way, saved changes to already-exported products are pushed automatically — see Webhooks & Real-Time Sync.

Status Conversion During Export

When products are exported to Shopify, their WooCommerce status (Published, Draft, Private) is translated into the corresponding Shopify status (ACTIVE, DRAFT, ARCHIVED) according to your Map Product Status rules.
