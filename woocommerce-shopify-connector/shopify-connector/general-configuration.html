General Configuration

Each connected store has its own configuration. Open the store's workspace (Manage Account), then go to Account Settings → General Configuration. Make your changes and select Save General Setting — "Settings updated successfully!" confirms the save.

Tips

If you submit the form without changing anything, the connector tells you: "Unable to update the settings because you have not change any data." That's informational, not an error.

Real-Time Sync – Fields to Update

Two multi-select lists control exactly which product fields real-time sync may overwrite:

  • Fields to Sync (WooCommerce → Shopify): Title, Description, Images, Price, Weight, Barcode, Product Status.
  • Fields to Sync (Shopify → WooCommerce): Title, Description, Images, Price, Categories, Weight, Barcode, Product Status.

When Product Status is selected, status modifications flow bidirectionally and are translated using your Map Product Status rules. Deselect any field you manage independently on one platform — for example, deselect Price in the Shopify → WooCommerce list if WooCommerce prices are set by your own pricing strategy, and automatic updates will leave them untouched.

Barcode / GTIN Synchronization

The connector supports end-to-end synchronization of product and variant barcodes between Shopify and WooCommerce.

SettingOptionsWhat it does
WooCommerce Barcode FieldWooCommerce Core GTIN / UPC (_global_unique_id) / Custom Meta Key (e.g. _barcode, _ean)Defines which field in WooCommerce stores barcode and GTIN values. Core GTIN is the native WooCommerce standard introduced in modern versions; choose Custom Meta Key if your store uses an extension or custom field to store barcodes.
Custom Barcode Meta KeyText input (e.g. _barcode)Only appears when Custom Meta Key is selected. Specify the exact postmeta key used to store product barcodes in your store.

Duplicate Conflict Prevention

To protect catalog integrity, the connector enforces barcode uniqueness. If an incoming Shopify barcode already exists on another WooCommerce product or variation, the connector logs a warning in the sync logs and skips updating the duplicate barcode, ensuring your product index remains valid.
