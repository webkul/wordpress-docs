Why the WCFM Shopify Connector is the complete vendor sync toolkit

Give every vendor their own Shopify connection inside the WCFM dashboard, and keep products, orders, stock and prices aligned in both directions.

Bidirectional Real Time Sync
Shopify webhooks and Action Scheduler keep prices, quantities, barcodes, and SKUs synchronized both ways, with baseline tracking eliminating double stock deduction.
Guided Setup Wizard
A four-step wizard takes each vendor from store name to a verified, authorized Shopify connection, with OAuth scope validation and copy-paste redirect URIs.
Multiple Shopify Stores
Every vendor can connect more than one Shopify account using OAuth or a legacy custom app, then switch between them from any tab with independent settings.
Import Products & Queue Card
Pull Shopify products by date range, ID, or full catalog into WooCommerce, monitored through an interactive Queue Card with status counters and retry controls.
Custom Shopify Listing Editor
Customize titles, descriptions, pricing strategies, barcodes, and drag-and-drop galleries specifically for Shopify without altering the WooCommerce catalog.
Import & Export Orders Queue
Manage sales bidirectionally with Action Scheduler queues, comprehensive status mapping, and customer fulfillment tracking displayed on marketplace order screens.
Multi-Row Category Mapping
Map multiple WooCommerce categories to Shopify collections with hierarchical breadcrumbs, conflict prevention, in-page validation, and unsaved changes discard modal.
Webhook Diagnostic & Repair
Test active Shopify webhook subscriptions against your site URL, repair broken endpoints with 1-click, and secure incoming requests with HMAC SHA-256 verification.
Administrator Oversight
See every vendor connection on one screen, monitor pending background sync notification queue depths, and disconnect accounts cleanly when vendors leave.
