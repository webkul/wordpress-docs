Real-Time Synchronization

Real-time synchronization keeps your WooCommerce store and connected Shopify stores seamlessly aligned without requiring manual user intervention. It operates via secure webhooks and background Action Scheduler workers, automatically updating prices, inventory levels, catalog details, and customer orders.

Core Prerequisites

For real-time synchronization to function properly:

  1. Active Shopify Webhooks: Ensure all required webhook subscriptions are registered and verified in Manage Account → Settings → Webhooks. See Webhooks.
  2. Auto-Sync Settings Enabled: Configure synchronization preferences in Manage Account → Settings → General Settings.
  3. Valid API Credentials: The connected account must maintain an active authorization token with required OAuth permissions.

Bidirectional Real-Time Product Sync ("Both Ways")

Product Auto-Sync supports four distinct operational modes:

ModeDirectionWhen It TriggersPrimary Use Case
DisableNoneReal-time product updates are suspended; manual import and export remain fully operational.Maintenance or staging periods.
Shopify to WooCommerceShopify → WooCommerceFired by PRODUCTS_UPDATE webhook whenever changes occur on Shopify.Shopify is the master catalog; marketplace is a sales channel.
WooCommerce to ShopifyWooCommerce → ShopifyFired whenever a vendor saves changes to a product in the WCFM dashboard.Marketplace is the master catalog where vendors manage products.
Both waysBidirectionalChanges made on either store automatically propagate to the other.Vendors actively managing inventory and catalog items from both dashboards.

Granular "Fields to Sync" Control

To protect custom content and prevent accidental overwrites, you can choose exactly which product attributes synchronize:

WooCommerce → Shopify Fields

Vendors can toggle: Title, Description, Images, Price, Categories, Weight, Barcode, SKU, Inventory, Status, and Tags.

Shopify → WooCommerce Fields

Vendors can toggle: Title, Description, Images, Price, Categories, Weight, Barcode, SKU, Inventory, Status, and Tags.

[!NOTE] If a field (e.g. Description) is deselected, vendors can customize product copy independently on WooCommerce without risk of Shopify webhooks reverting their edits.

Real-Time Barcode Synchronization

The connector includes native bidirectional Barcode synchronization. Barcodes (UPC, EAN, ISBN, or alphanumeric barcodes) assigned in WooCommerce synchronize directly to Shopify variant barcode fields, and changes in Shopify update WooCommerce barcode metadata in real time.

Automated Product Creation & Deletion

Auto Import Product (Shopify → WooCommerce)

When enabled in General Settings, the PRODUCTS_CREATE webhook automatically creates a new WooCommerce product whenever a new item is published on Shopify, assigning your default category, price rules, and image settings.

Auto Export Product (WooCommerce → Shopify)

When enabled, newly created or published WooCommerce products are automatically enqueued and exported to your Shopify store in the background. Products that are already linked or imported from Shopify are skipped to prevent duplicates.

Bidirectional Auto-Delete Rules

  • Auto Delete WooCommerce Product on Shopify Deletion: Choose between Disable, Move to Trash, or Permanently Delete. Triggers when a linked Shopify product is deleted and Auto-Sync is set to Shopify to WooCommerce or Both ways.
  • Auto Delete Shopify Product on WooCommerce Deletion: When enabled, permanently deleting a product in WooCommerce permanently removes the corresponding item from Shopify (requires Auto-Sync to be WooCommerce to Shopify or Both ways).

Location-Based Stock Sync & Baseline Tracking

To prevent inventory discrepancies during peak shopping periods, the connector includes location-based stock baseline tracking:

  • Stock changes are tracked relative to a recorded baseline for each designated Shopify location.
  • Concurrent customer checkouts on WooCommerce and webhook inventory updates from Shopify do not double-deduct inventory.
  • Out-of-stock items update immediately across both platforms.

Automated Order Synchronization

  • Auto Import Order (Realtime): Automatically pulls new Shopify customer purchases into WooCommerce via webhooks in real time.
  • Shopify Export Order: Automatically transmits WooCommerce orders to Shopify in Realtime upon order placement or upon entering a designated status (e.g., Processing).
  • Order Updates Sync: Bidirectional synchronization pushes cancellations, payment updates, and tracking changes back and forth between stores.
  • Imported Order Status Sync: Pushes order fulfillment and status transitions made in WCFM back to Shopify.
  • Fulfillment & Tracking Updates: When an order is marked fulfilled in Shopify, carrier and tracking information flows directly onto the customer's WooCommerce order.

HMAC SHA-256 Webhook Security

Every incoming Shopify webhook payload is validated against your store's secret key using HMAC SHA-256 cryptographic signatures. Unsigned or spoofed requests are rejected immediately, protecting your catalog and inventory from unauthorized modifications.

Troubleshooting Real-Time Sync

If automated updates are not reflecting:

  1. Verify Webhook Health: Open Settings → Webhooks and run the 1-Click Repair Webhooks diagnostic to verify subscriptions.
  2. Check Account Connection: Ensure the account list shows Connected.
  3. Review Fields to Sync: Check that the specific field (e.g., Price or Inventory) is selected under Fields to Sync in General Settings.
  4. Confirm Auto-Sync Direction: Verify that Product Auto-Sync is set to Shopify to WooCommerce, WooCommerce to Shopify, or Both ways.
  5. Inspect Background Queue: Check the Queue Card on product or order tabs for pending or failed Action Scheduler workers.
