Export Products

Exporting pushes your WooCommerce products to Shopify, allowing you to list and sell your marketplace catalog across your Shopify storefronts and sales channels.

Open Manage Account → Products → Export Product.

What Can Be Exported

The Export table displays all eligible WooCommerce products owned by your vendor account, including both simple products and variable products with variations.

ColumnInformation & Actions
Thumbnail & TitleProduct thumbnail, WooCommerce ID, product type, and action links: Edit, Edit Shopify Listing, and Delete.
Export StatusLive status badge indicating whether the item is Exported or Not Exported. For exported items, displays a direct link to the product in the Shopify Admin (https://<store>.myshopify.com/admin/products/<id>).
Price & StockCurrent WooCommerce catalog pricing and stock levels.
Exclusion SwitchOne-click toggle switch to instantly exclude or include the product from automatic or bulk export.
Row Details ToggleExpands variant matrices, category mappings, and export timestamps.

[!NOTE] Imported products are excluded: Products originally imported from Shopify do not appear in the Export table. This prevents products from being circular-exported back to their origin store.

Dedicated "Edit Shopify Listing" Interface

The connector features a dedicated Shopify Listing Editor that allows vendors to customize product listings specifically for Shopify without modifying their master WooCommerce catalog.

Vendors can maintain store-specific titles, descriptions, pricing strategies, barcodes, and curated galleries for each connected Shopify store. For example, if a vendor connects two Shopify accounts (a retail storefront and an outlet store), each store can maintain independent listing overrides.

To open the editor, navigate to Manage Account → Products → Export Product and select Edit Shopify Listing on any product row.

Tab 1: General Information

The General tab configures core storefront listing attributes and rich product descriptions:

  • Title: Provide a custom, SEO-optimized title for Shopify shoppers. By default, it inherits the WooCommerce product title (Following WooCommerce). Click Use WooCommerce value to reset the field back to the catalog baseline.
  • SKU (Simple Products): Enter a custom SKU specifically for the Shopify listing without changing the WooCommerce SKU.
  • Tags: Enter comma-separated product tags (e.g., snowboard, winter, sale). These tags sync directly to Shopify and power automated collections and storefront search filtering.
  • Shopify Status: Choose whether the listing publishes to Shopify as Active, Draft, or Archived.
  • Barcodes Repeater: Add and classify up to 20 industry-standard product identifiers per listing, including UPC, EAN, ISBN, GTIN, ASIN, or NS-PID.
  • Description: A dedicated rich-text WYSIWYG editor (with Visual and Code / HTML tabs) allows vendors to format customized product copy, embed bulleted specifications, and format promotional text tailored for Shopify buyers without altering the WooCommerce description.

Tab 2: Product Details & Pricing Strategy

The Product Details tab manages mapping metadata, custom pricing rules, and inventory tracking modes:

Product Mapping Identifiers

  • WooCommerce Product ID: Internal catalog ID of the WooCommerce product.
  • Shopify Product ID: Remote Shopify product GID (or marked Unmapped (Export Candidate) before initial export).
  • Mapping Source: The channel that created the mapping (Export, Import, or Manual).
  • Product Type: WooCommerce product structure (Simple or Variable).

Pricing & Quantity Controls

  • Pricing Strategy: Choose how the Shopify price is calculated:
    • Use WooCommerce price: Dynamically inherits current WooCommerce price.
    • Fixed price override: Enter a dedicated fixed price for Shopify.
    • Plus/Minus adjustment: Add or subtract a fixed amount or percentage specifically for Shopify.
  • Global Price Rules Toggle: Choose whether account-wide Price Rules run after this listing's pricing strategy when syncing to Shopify.
  • Compare At Price: Set an original strikethrough price on Shopify to display promotional markdowns and discount badges.
  • Shopify Stock / Quantity: Choose whether Shopify stock mirrors WooCommerce inventory or uses a dedicated fixed allocation.
  • Weight: Override shipping weight for accurate carrier calculation on Shopify checkouts.

Custom Shopify Media & Gallery Curation

Vendors can curate product photography specifically for Shopify without changing their WooCommerce product gallery:

  • Image Source: Switch between Standard (WooCommerce Featured Image & Gallery) and Custom Shopify Gallery (Curate & Reorder).
  • Add Images: Select + Add Image from Media Library to select high-resolution images from the WordPress Media Library. External URLs are not permitted.
  • Drag-and-Drop Reordering: Drag image thumbnails into any sequence. The first image automatically receives the Main badge and serves as Shopify's primary featured image (Position 0).
  • Removing Images: Click the × button on any thumbnail to detach it from the Shopify listing. The underlying media file remains safely stored in your WordPress Media Library.

Tab 3: Managing Variable Products ("Variations")

When editing a variable product (such as apparel with multiple sizes and colors), an accordion-based Variations tab appears between General and Product Details:

Select any variation row to expand its override panel:

  • Shopify Variant ID: Remote GraphQL GID for the Shopify variant.
  • Variation Image: Assign a specific photo from the WordPress Media Library to this variation on Shopify.
  • Pricing Strategy: Inherit the parent product's pricing strategy or configure an independent variant-level price.
  • Compare At Price: Set promotional sale strikethrough prices per variation.
  • SKU & Barcodes: Assign variant-specific SKUs and barcode classifications.
  • Weight: Override individual variation shipping weights for carrier calculations.

Tab 4: Inventory & Synchronization Status

The Inventory & Status tab provides transparent diagnostic visibility into fulfillment locations and background synchronization checkpoints:

  • Location Stock: Displays the selected Shopify inventory location (e.g., India Warehouse) and its current inventory policy. Click Change in Store Settings → to switch locations.
  • Auto-Sync Direction: Displays the account's active synchronization policy (e.g., Both Way (Active) or WooCommerce to Shopify).
  • Allowlisted Sync Fields: Pill badges list which product attributes (title, description, image, price, categories, weight, barcode, sku, inventory, status, tags) are authorized for automatic real-time sync.
  • Revisions Checkpoint Grid:
    • Desired Revision: Counter tracking every local save.
    • Applied Revision: The latest revision confirmed and applied to Shopify.
    • Attempted Revision: The latest revision attempted by background sync workers.
    • Sync Status: Real-time state (Applied, Staged, Queued, or Failed).
  • Retry Sync Button: If a network failure occurs, select Retry Sync to re-queue unapplied listing changes immediately.

Saving and Resetting Overrides

  • Save Shopify Listing:
    • Click Save Shopify Listing at the bottom of the editor.
    • Mapped Listings with Auto-Sync Active: The connector persists changes locally and enqueues an asynchronous update to Shopify.
    • Unmapped Listings or Inactive Auto-Sync: Overrides are safely persisted locally as Staged and are applied during the next export.
  • Reset to Defaults:
    • Click Reset to Defaults in the bottom-right footer to open the safety confirmation modal.
    • Confirming reverts all custom titles, descriptions, pricing, barcodes, and media back to inheriting directly from the WooCommerce catalog baseline without altering any WordPress media or catalog data.

How to Export Products

Manual Bulk Export

  1. Select the checkboxes next to the products you want to export.
  2. Select Export from the Bulk actions dropdown.
  3. Click Apply.

The connector packages the product data, attributes, categories, and images, and queues them into the background Action Scheduler queue. The Queue Card displays real-time progress, allowing you to monitor batch execution or safely navigate away.

Automatic Export ("Auto Export Product")

In General Settings, enable Auto Export Product. When enabled, newly published WooCommerce products are automatically enqueued and exported to Shopify in the background without requiring manual bulk action.

Preventing Duplicate Products on Export

In General Settings, configure Prevent Duplicate Products (Export):

  • Create new product (Default): Always creates a new Shopify item even if a matching SKU exists on Shopify.
  • Update product if SKU exists: When a matching SKU is detected on Shopify, the connector maps the existing Shopify item to this WooCommerce product and updates its details, eliminating duplicates.

Product Exclusion System

To prevent certain products from being exported to Shopify:

  • Quick Table Switch: Click the Exclusion Switch icon on any product row to toggle its export eligibility instantly.
  • Settings Exclude Products: Navigate to Settings → Exclude Products to search and manage the master excluded products list.
  • Excluded products are bypassed by both manual bulk exports and the Auto Export Product background worker.
