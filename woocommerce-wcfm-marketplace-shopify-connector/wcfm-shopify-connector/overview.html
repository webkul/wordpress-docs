Overview

The connector links a vendor's Shopify store to your WooCommerce marketplace so the same catalog and customer orders can live in both places without being maintained twice.

How the Connection is Organised

Everything operates through a Shopify account — a connected Shopify store. A vendor can connect multiple Shopify stores, and each store carries its own independent settings, category mappings, price rules, status mappings, and webhooks. Switching between accounts using the store switcher never mixes their configuration.

Each account is managed from Vendor Dashboard → Shopify Connector → Manage Account, which is organised into six top-level tabs:

TabPurpose & Features
AccountStore name, domain, API credentials, account type, OAuth scope status, and store switcher.
Map CategoriesMulti-row interface to match Shopify collections to store categories with hierarchical breadcrumbs.
ProductsImport products from Shopify and export products to Shopify, monitored via the background Queue Card.
OrdersImport orders from Shopify and export orders to Shopify with live Action Scheduler progress.
SettingsGeneral Configuration, Product Status Mapping, Order Status Mapping, Webhooks Diagnostic, and Exclude Products.
Price RuleAutomated percentage or fixed-amount price adjustments by price bracket.

Which Way Does Data Move?

The connector works bidirectionally, and vendors have complete control over sync directions and granular fields.

Shopify → WooCommerce

  • Import Products: Pulls a Shopify catalog in and creates WooCommerce products via non-blocking background queues.
  • Import Orders: Pulls Shopify customer orders in and creates WooCommerce orders with commission calculations.
  • Auto Import Product: Creates a WooCommerce product automatically when a new item is listed on Shopify.
  • Product Auto-Sync: Keeps price, quantity, barcode, and SKU current in WooCommerce.

WooCommerce → Shopify

  • Export Products: Pushes the vendor's WooCommerce products to Shopify, with optional custom overrides via the Shopify Listing Editor.
  • Auto Export Product: Automatically exports newly published WooCommerce products to Shopify in the background.
  • Export Orders: Sends WooCommerce orders to Shopify, either manually in bulk or automatically by order status.
  • Product Auto-Sync: Pushes product edits made in the vendor dashboard to Shopify in real time.

Bidirectional Synchronization ("Both Ways")

Product Auto-Sync supports Both ways. Changes made on either Shopify or WooCommerce automatically propagate to the other. To protect specific content from accidental overwrites, vendors can use Fields to Sync to choose exactly which fields synchronize automatically.

What Makes Real-Time Work

Manual imports and exports always work on their own. Real-time background updates rely on webhooks registered on the Shopify store and verified via HMAC SHA-256 signatures.

Webhooks are audited and repaired in one click from Settings → Webhooks, and cover five core events: order creation, order updates, product creation, product updates, and product deletion. See Webhooks and Real-Time Sync.

Imported and Exported Products Are Kept Apart

A product that was imported from Shopify does not appear in the Export Product list. The two lists deliberately never overlap, preventing circular loops and accidental re-exporting.
