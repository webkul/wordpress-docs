WCFM Marketplace Shopify Connector

Version 3.0.0 · by Webkul

The WCFM Marketplace Shopify Connector lets each vendor on your WCFM marketplace connect their own Shopify store (or several) and keep products, orders and inventory aligned with WooCommerce — in both directions.

Every vendor works only with their own Shopify accounts. Nothing a vendor imports, exports or configures is visible to another vendor.

Who does what

Vendors do nearly all the work, from Vendor Dashboard → Shopify Connector: connect stores, map collections to categories, import and export products and orders, set price rules, and control sync behaviour per store.

Administrators oversee rather than operate. From Webkul WC Addons → WCFM Shopify Connector they can see every vendor's connected store, its live connection status and whether its webhooks are in place, and disconnect an account if needed.
