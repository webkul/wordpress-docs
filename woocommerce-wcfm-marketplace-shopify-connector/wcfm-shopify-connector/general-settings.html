General Settings

These settings control how a Shopify store synchronises with WooCommerce. Settings are configured and saved per Shopify account — connecting additional Shopify stores gives each store its own independent configuration.

Open Manage Account → Settings. The Settings screen is organized into five dedicated sub-tabs:

  • General Settings — core catalog defaults, duplicate handling, auto-sync, auto-delete, and fields to update.
  • Product Status Mapping — maps Shopify lifecycle statuses (Active, Draft, Archived) to WooCommerce post statuses.
  • Order Status Mapping — bidirectional order lifecycle, fulfillment, and financial status mappings.
  • Webhooks — webhook registration, HMAC SHA-256 verification, and 1-click diagnostic repair.
  • Exclude Products — selective product exclusion from export and import.

Select Save Changes at the bottom of the form when you are done; a green confirmation banner confirms Settings updated successfully.

[!IMPORTANT] SKUs Must Match Exactly on Both Platforms For Prevent Duplicate Products to link and update existing items, the SKU must be exactly identical on both WooCommerce and Shopify. If an item lacks an SKU or if SKUs differ in casing or spacing, a new product is created instead.

[!TIP] Choosing In The Background accelerates large catalog imports. Products are created instantly so your store catalog is immediately functional, while high-resolution media attachments download asynchronously in background batches.

Export Order Settings

Configure financial calculation and fee handling when exporting orders from WooCommerce to Shopify:

SettingOptionsEffect
Export Order ShippingInclude Shipping Cost · Exclude Shipping CostPasses shipping line items and shipping methods to the Shopify order.
Export Order TaxInclude Tax Cost · Exclude Tax CostTransmits calculated WooCommerce tax rates and tax amounts to Shopify.
Export Order DiscountInclude Discount · Exclude DiscountReflects applied coupon codes and item discounts in the exported Shopify order.

Match these settings to your Shopify store's accounting configuration so financial reports and invoice totals reconcile accurately across both systems.

Real-Time Auto-Syncing & Deletion Settings

Configure real-time background synchronization between WooCommerce and Shopify.

SettingOptionsWhat it does
Product Auto-SyncDisable · Shopify to WooCommerce · WooCommerce to Shopify · Both waysControls the direction of live product updates. Setting Both ways keeps catalog changes synchronised bidirectionally in real time.
Shopify Export OrderManual · Realtime · Specific WooCommerce Order StatusDecides when WooCommerce orders are automatically pushed to Shopify: Manual disables auto-export; Realtime pushes the order to Shopify immediately upon creation; or choose a status trigger (such as Processing or Completed) to export only when the order reaches that status.
Auto Import ProductEnable · DisableAutomatically creates a new WooCommerce product whenever a new item is published on Shopify (PRODUCTS_CREATE).
Auto Export ProductEnable · DisableAutomatically exports newly published WooCommerce products to Shopify in the background. Products already linked to Shopify are skipped.
Auto Import OrderEnable · DisableAutomatically imports new Shopify customer orders into WooCommerce via webhooks in real time as soon as checkout completes on Shopify.
Order Updates to ShopifyEnable · DisableEnqueues mapped status actions (cancellations, marking paid, tracking numbers, fulfillments) to Shopify for orders originally exported from WooCommerce.
Order Updates from ShopifyEnable · DisableAccepts Shopify ORDERS_UPDATED webhook notifications and applies eligible status changes to the corresponding WooCommerce orders.
Imported Order Status SyncEnable · DisableEnqueues fulfillment and status updates back to Shopify when a vendor changes the status of a WooCommerce order originally imported from Shopify.
Auto Delete WooCommerce Product on Shopify DeletionDisable · Move to Trash · Permanently DeleteAutomatically handles the WooCommerce product when its linked item is deleted on Shopify (applies when Auto-Sync is Shopify to WooCommerce or Both ways).
Auto Delete Shopify Product on WooCommerce DeletionDisable · EnablePermanently deletes the corresponding Shopify product when a vendor permanently deletes a WooCommerce product (applies when Auto-Sync is WooCommerce to Shopify or Both ways).

Real-Time Sync — Fields to Update

To prevent unintended overwrites and protect custom modifications, the connector provides granular multi-select controls so vendors can choose exactly which fields update automatically in each direction:

  • WooCommerce → Shopify: Select any combination of Title, Description, Images, Price, Categories, Weight, Barcode, SKU, Inventory, Status, and Tags.
  • Shopify → WooCommerce: Select any combination of Title, Description, Images, Price, Categories, Weight, Barcode, SKU, Inventory, Status, and Tags.

[!NOTE] Barcode synchronization is fully bidirectional and integrates with WooCommerce barcode meta and Shopify variant barcode fields.

Product Status Mapping

Open Settings → Product Status Mapping to configure how product lifecycle states translate between Shopify and WooCommerce.

At the top of the screen, an Active Synchronization Settings banner highlights the current Product Auto-Sync direction and confirms whether status synchronization is active in your Fields to Sync configuration.

Shopify StatusDescriptionMapped WooCommerce StatusDefault Value
ActiveProduct is published, ready for sale, and visible on sales channels.Published · Draft · Pending Review · PrivatePublished
DraftProduct is unfinished or hidden from public sales channels.Published · Draft · Pending Review · PrivateDraft
ArchivedProduct is discontinued or archived from catalog.Published · Draft · Pending Review · PrivateDraft

Select Save Status Mappings to persist changes, or Reset to Defaults to restore standard WooCommerce lifecycle associations.

Order Status Mapping

Open Settings → Order Status Mapping to configure status translations for orders flowing between Shopify and WooCommerce.

The status dependency banner displays live badges for Auto Import Order, Imported Order Status Sync, and Order Updates to Shopify.

1. Order Lifecycle Status

  • Cancelled: Translates Shopify cancelled orders to WooCommerce (default: Cancelled).

2. Fulfillment Statuses

  • Fulfilled: All line items shipped on Shopify (default: Completed).
  • Unfulfilled: Order placed, awaiting dispatch (default: Processing).
  • Partially Fulfilled: Partial shipment dispatched (default: Processing).
  • On Hold: Fulfillment paused on Shopify (default: On hold).
  • Restocked: Items returned and restocked in inventory (default: Refunded).

3. Financial Statuses

  • Paid: Payment captured in full (default: Processing).
  • Pending Payment: COD, bank wire, or invoice pending (default: Pending payment).
  • Authorized: Card authorization held (default: On hold).
  • Partially Paid: Partial deposit collected (default: On hold).
  • Refunded: Order refunded in full (default: Refunded).
  • Partially Refunded: Partial credit issued (default: Refunded).
  • Voided: Authorization voided prior to capture (default: Cancelled).

Exclude Products

Open Settings → Exclude Products to prevent specific catalog items from synchronizing.

1. WooCommerce to Shopify Product Exclusions (Export)

  • Use the live AJAX product search selector to find WooCommerce products by title or ID.
  • Select Add to Exclude List to block them from bulk exports and real-time syncing.
  • The table displays all currently excluded products with image thumbnail, title, ID, and SKU.
  • Remove products individually or via Remove Selected / Allow Export bulk action.

2. Shopify to WooCommerce Product Exclusions (Import)

  • Shopify Product IDs: Type or paste numeric Shopify IDs into the tag-box input and press Enter or comma.
  • Shopify SKUs: Enter exact SKUs to prevent unwanted vendor inventory or discontinued lines from being imported into WooCommerce.
  • Select Add to Exclude List to save the rule.

Sensible Recommended Configuration

For a marketplace vendor using Shopify as their primary inventory master:

  1. Products to Import: All products
  2. Products to Import having Shopify Status: Active
  3. Import Only In-Stock Products: Enable
  4. Prevent Duplicate Products (Import & Export): Update product if SKU exist and product not synced (ensure SKUs are identical on both platforms)
  5. Product Auto-Sync: Both ways
  6. Auto Export Product: Enable
  7. Auto Import Product: Enable
  8. Shopify Export Order: Realtime (or Processing to export only after payment capture)
  9. Order Updates to Shopify & From Shopify: Enable
  10. Imported Order Status Sync: Enable
