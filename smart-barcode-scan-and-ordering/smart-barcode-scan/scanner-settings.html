Scanner Settings

The Scanner Settings tab controls how barcodes are matched and how the console behaves while your staff scan. Go to Webkul WC Addons → Smart Barcode Scan → Scanner Settings.

Make your changes and click Save Settings. A confirmation — Settings Saved! — appears and the new settings take effect immediately, including in any scan popup that is already open.

Barcode Search Fields

These decide which product fields a scanned code is checked against. Tick every field your labels actually use; ticking fields you do not use only slows lookups down.

SettingDescriptionDefault
Product IDMatch the scanned value against the WooCommerce product ID. Only applied when the value is numericOff
Product SKUMatch the scanned value against the product SKU, exactlyOn
Global Unique Identifier (GTIN, UPC, EAN, or ISBN)Match against WooCommerce's global unique identifier field — the one shown as GTIN, UPC, EAN, or ISBN on the product's Inventory tabOff
Custom Meta Field KeyThe meta key of any other field that holds your barcode, for example one added by an import or another plugin. Leave blank if you do not have oneEmpty

Fields are searched in the order listed above, and the first match wins.

Note

A search by product name always runs after these fields, whether or not anything is ticked. That is why typing a product name into the scan box finds the product even with every checkbox cleared.

Tips

Custom Meta Field Key is also used to look up orders. If your orders carry a barcode or consignment number in a custom field, entering that key here lets staff scan it to open the order.

Choosing the right fields

Your labels containTick
The SKU printed as a barcodeProduct SKU
A retail EAN / UPC / GTIN barcode from the manufacturerGlobal Unique Identifier
The WooCommerce product ID, printed in-houseProduct ID
A code stored by another plugin or an importEnter its meta key in Custom Meta Field Key

Console Preferences

SettingDescriptionDefault
Audio FeedbackPlay a tone after each scan — one sound for a match, another for a failure. Lets operators keep their eyes on the shelfOn
Auto Clear InputEmpty the scan box automatically after each successful scan, so the next item can be scanned immediatelyOff
Add to Cart After SearchAdd every scanned product to the Create Order cart automatically, with a quantity of 1Off
Activity LoggingRecord scans and changes in the Activity Logs tabOn
Log History LimitHow many log entries to keep. Accepts 10–500100

When to change them

  • Audio Feedback — leave on for shelf and packing-bench work. Turn it off in a quiet showroom, or mute it for one session with the speaker button in the scan popup.
  • Auto Clear Input — turn on for stock counts and any workflow where staff scan many items in a row.
  • Add to Cart After Search — turn on for a till or trade counter, where almost every scan is a sale. Leave off for stock work, or every product you look at ends up in the cart.
  • Activity Logging — leave on. Turning it off stops new entries being recorded but keeps the ones you already have.
  • Log History Limit — raise it if you review scan history days later; lower it if you only ever check the last few actions.

Important

Log History Limit must be between 10 and 500. The form will not submit a value outside that range.

Where these settings apply

Every setting applies to the whole site, not to individual users, and takes effect for:

  • The Scan Console tab.
  • The scan popup, including its Inventory, Orders and Create Order tabs.
  • The quick Barcode Scanner in the WordPress toolbar.

Saving settings is itself recorded in Activity Logs under the source Settings, with every value listed — so you can see when a barcode field was turned off and by whom.
