Why the WooCommerce AliExpress Dropship runs your dropshipping store end to end

Bulk AliExpress ImportTick products on any AliExpress listing or search page with the Chrome extension and import up to fifty in one run, with live progress and a clear summary.Link, ID and CSV ImportPaste AliExpress product links or IDs, or upload a CSV file of up to five hundred products with your own names, prices, and SEO text, right from the WordPress admin.Native WooCommerce ProductsCreate simple and variable WooCommerce products with images, variations, SKU, stock, weight, attributes, and SEO meta from every imported AliExpress item in a single bulk action.Direct API OrderingPlace customer orders on AliExpress through the AliExpress Open API without leaving WordPress, review the shipping address and items, and choose the shipping carrier for each order.Extension Cart FillingPrefer to check out yourself? The Chrome extension opens each ordered product, selects the right option and quantity, adds it to your AliExpress cart, and shows the delivery address.Automatic Price MarkupSet selling prices on import as the AliExpress price, a custom price, or a percentage increase or decrease, and refresh price and stock automatically with scheduled auto sync.Warehouses and ManagersCreate warehouses, invite a warehouse manager for each one, and mass assign products, so every supplier sees only their own products, orders, and income in a restricted dashboard.Filtered Review ImportBring AliExpress buyer reviews into your product pages, filtered by star rating, with empty reviews skipped and optional draft moderation before anything is published to shoppers.Commission and PayoutsTake a fixed or percentage commission on warehouse sales by cart total, track payable and paid amounts per warehouse, and keep a searchable log of every payout you record.WooCommerce AliExpress Dropship ComparisonSee how this plugin compares with ALD, AliDropship Woo, AliNext, and DSers across import methods, AliExpress API ordering, warehouses with their own managers, supplier payouts, and licensing model.Dropship Help CenterFind setup guides, import and ordering how tos, troubleshooting fixes, an exact error message index, requirements, and support contacts, organized by role and by task so you reach the right page in one click.
