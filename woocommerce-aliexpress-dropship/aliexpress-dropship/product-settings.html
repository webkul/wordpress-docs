Product Setting

Product Setting controls how imported AliExpress products are created and updated in WooCommerce. The same options appear in step 4 of the Setup Wizard.

Go to Webkul WC Addons → Warehouse Manager → Dropship Settings → Product Setting.

Settings

SettingDescriptionDefault
Default CategoryWooCommerce category given to imported products.First category
Enable/Disable AliExpress Categories importEnable creates the product's AliExpress categories in WooCommerce and files the product under them instead of the default category.—
Enable/Disable Auto SyncingEnable refreshes warehouse-assigned products from AliExpress on a schedule.Disable
Default Time Interval for Auto Sync (in hours)Hours between automatic refreshes. Appears when auto syncing is enabled.—
Default Product StockStock quantity used when AliExpress reports no stock for a product.Empty
Default Product StatusPublish, Pending Review, or Draft.Publish
Default Product VisibilityShop and search results, Shop only, Search results only, or Hidden.Shop and search results
Default Product WarehouseWarehouse new products are assigned to automatically when created.None
Default Product Price configurationHow the selling price is set — see below.Same as AliExpress
Default Product LanguageLanguage product details are fetched in.English
Default CurrencyCurrency prices are fetched in.US Dollar
Import Product DescriptionImports the AliExpress description and specifications. Off means imported products have no description.Off
Do not import images from the descriptionImports the description text without its images.Off

Select Save Product Settings.

Price configuration

OptionSelling price
Same as AliExpressRegular price = AliExpress price; sale price = AliExpress sale price.
Custom PriceEvery product and variation gets the fixed amount you enter.
Increase PriceAliExpress prices raised by the percentage you enter.
Decrease PriceAliExpress prices lowered by the percentage you enter.
  • The rule is applied when a product is created and every time it is updated from AliExpress.
  • An empty or zero amount leaves the AliExpress price unchanged.
  • A price you type in the Chrome extension's edit dialog or in a CSV file overrides the rule for that product.
  • Prices are fetched in the Default Currency and are not converted to your store currency, so set it to match your store.

Not the same as Pricing Rules

The Pricing Rules tab sets your commission on warehouse sales, not selling prices. See Pricing Rules.

Auto sync

When auto syncing is enabled, the plugin periodically refreshes products assigned to a warehouse, updating their name, description, categories, price, stock, images, and variations from AliExpress. Products that are not assigned to any warehouse are not auto synced — refresh those with Update From AliExpress on the Imported Products List.
