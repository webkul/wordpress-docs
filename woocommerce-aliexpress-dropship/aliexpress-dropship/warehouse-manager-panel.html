Warehouse Manager Panel

Warehouse managers sign in at your normal WordPress login page with the username and the password from their welcome email. They get the same Webkul WC Addons → Warehouse Manager screen as the administrator, limited to their own warehouse.

What a manager can see and do

AreaManager access
Warehouse ManagerTheir own account only.
Warehouse ListTheir own warehouse, which they can Edit but not delete.
Products → Assigned ProductsProducts assigned to their warehouse, including Sync Reviews.
AliExpressThe AliExpress product list, with View Product only.
Pricing RulesView only.
OrdersOrders containing their warehouse's products.
IncomeTheir own income, payable, and paid amounts, without the Pay action.
Transaction LogPayment Logs and Order Logs.
Import Products, Dropship SettingsNot available.

Managers cannot add or delete warehouses or managers, import products, change settings, or record payouts.

Placing orders

The Place Order and Cancel Order buttons are visible to managers on the Orders screen. Decide in your team who places AliExpress orders — the order is paid from the AliExpress account connected under Account Setting.
