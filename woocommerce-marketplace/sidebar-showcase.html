Products Moderation & Management

Review, approve, assign, and edit products submitted by all marketplace vendors directly from the administrative catalog control center.

Exact WP-Admin Menu Match
This documentation section strictly mirrors the Marketplace > Products submenu (admin.php?page=wk-marketplace-products) in your WordPress admin dashboard.

Admin Product Management Workflow

1

Access the Products Table

Navigate to Marketplace > Products in the WordPress admin menu.

  • View complete listing of all items across all registered sellers.
  • Filter products by Vendor Name, Product Status (Online, Pending, Draft), or Product Category.
  • Search specific items by title or SKU.
2

Inspect & Approve Pending Submissions

When "Allow Seller to Publish" is disabled in settings, newly created products require your review:

  • Click Edit or Quick Edit on the pending product row.
  • Verify product imagery, price, inventory levels, and downloadable files.
  • Change status from Pending to Publish to make it live in the store.
3

Assign Existing Store Products to Vendors

Promote or re-attribute products between vendors or from admin to vendor:

  • Select the product checkbox, select Assign to Vendor, pick the seller, and click Apply.
Bulk Actions Available
You can perform batch operations on multiple products simultaneously: Approve, Delete, or Assign Categories directly from the bulk dropdown menu.
