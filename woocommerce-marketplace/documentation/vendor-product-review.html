Vendor Product Reviews

In WooCommerce Multi-Vendor Marketplace, customer reviews on individual products provide direct proof of buyer satisfaction and product quality. Positive product reviews help boost your catalog ranking, build shopper confidence, and drive higher sales conversion.

The Product Reviews console in the Seller Dashboard gives vendors a dedicated place to monitor all customer ratings and written feedback left for specific items in their catalog. Sellers can quickly find reviews with keyword search, sort feedback by newest or oldest, and apply detailed filters by customer, star rating, moderation status, or date ranges.

Accessing Product Reviews in the Seller Dashboard

Vendors can navigate to the Product Reviews screen through the seller panel sidebar:

  1. Sign in to your Seller Account.
  2. In the left navigation menu, locate the Performance section.
  3. Click Product Reviews to open the review management screen.
Access DetailValue / PathDescription
Direct URL/seller/seller-product-reviews/Direct web address to your product reviews console.
Menu GroupPerformanceFound in the main seller navigation sidebar alongside Account Health and Seller Reviews.
ScopeCatalog Product FeedbackDisplays ratings and reviews submitted by customers specifically for your products.

Accessing on Mobile or Small Screens

When browsing the seller dashboard on a mobile phone or tablet, tap the Open menu hamburger button in the top navigation bar to expand the sidebar. Then open Performance and tap Product Reviews.

Top Controls and Quick Search

At the top of the Product Reviews list, you have quick controls to organize your view and locate specific buyer feedback without opening the full filter drawer:

1. Search Reviews Bar

The search box allows you to find feedback instantly:

  • Search terms: Type a customer name (e.g., Demo Customer), a product title keyword (e.g., Tee, Watch, Runner), or specific words mentioned in the review description (e.g., breathable, comfortable, quality).
  • Press Enter: Hit Enter on your keyboard to run the search. The table updates immediately to display only matching reviews.

2. Items Per Page Selector

Choose how many review records you want to see per page. Available display densities include:

  • 5
  • 10 (Default)
  • 20
  • 30
  • 40
  • 50

Selecting a new number automatically refreshes the table to show that number of rows per page.

3. Sort By Dropdown

Organize the order in which reviews appear on your screen:

  • Newest first (Default): Keeps your most recent customer feedback at the very top.
  • Oldest first: Sorts records starting from your earliest catalog reviews.

4. Filter Button

Click the Filter button to slide open the comprehensive Apply Filters drawer from the right side of your screen.

Understanding the Product Reviews Table

The table layout presents three clear columns designed to give you complete visibility into who reviewed your product, which item was reviewed, and what rating they gave:

ColumnInformation DisplayedWhat It Tells You
Customer / EmailCustomer Name, Email, & Verified BadgeShows the buyer's full name, registered email address, and an avatar. If the customer purchased the item through the marketplace, a green Verified owner badge appears.
Product / Date / StatusProduct Link, Submission Date, & Status BadgeDisplays the exact product title with a direct link to the storefront product page, the date the review was submitted (e.g., August 20, 2026), and the moderation status badge (Approved or Pending).
Rating / DescriptionStar Score & Review CommentDisplays the numerical star score (e.g., Rated 4 out of 5 or Rated 5 out of 5), visual gold star icons, and the full comment written by the buyer.

Key Badges Explained

  • Verified owner: Confirms that the reviewer purchased this exact item from your store in a completed order. Reviews with this badge carry higher trust among prospective buyers.
  • Approved: The review has passed marketplace moderation and is publicly visible on your product page for all storefront visitors to see.
  • Pending: The review is awaiting administrator moderation or verification. Pending reviews are not yet visible to the public on the storefront product page until approved.

Direct Product Link

You can click the product name directly in the Product / Date / Status column to open the live product page on your storefront in a new tab. This lets you see exactly how the review appears to customers.

Using the Product Reviews Filter Drawer

When you have multiple products and numerous reviews, the slide-out filter drawer helps you quickly find reviews matching specific criteria:

To use the filter drawer:

  1. Click the Filter button above the reviews table.
  2. The Apply Filters drawer will slide in from the right side of the screen.
  3. Configure any combination of the following filter options:

1. Customer Name

Enter a customer's first name, last name, or username into the Customer field to see reviews submitted by that specific buyer.

2. Customer Email

Enter an email address into the Email field to find all feedback submitted from that buyer account.

3. Date Presets (Quick Select)

Select one of the convenient radio buttons to filter reviews within a standard calendar time frame:

  • Today: Feedback submitted during the current day.
  • Yesterday: Feedback submitted on the previous day.
  • This Week: Reviews submitted during the current calendar week.
  • This Month: Reviews submitted during the current month.
  • Last Month: Reviews from the previous full calendar month.
  • Last 3 Months: Reviews submitted over the previous 90 days.
  • Last 6 Months: Feedback submitted across the previous half-year.
  • This Year: All reviews submitted during the current year.

4. Custom Date Range

If you want to view reviews from a specific marketing campaign, seasonal sale, or promotion period:

  • From date: Select or enter the starting calendar date.
  • To date: Select or enter the ending calendar date.

5. Review Status

Filter reviews according to their moderation status:

  • Select: Shows all reviews regardless of status.
  • Approved: Displays only reviews currently published and visible to buyers on the storefront.
  • Pending: Displays reviews waiting for marketplace moderation.

6. Star Rating

Filter reviews by customer satisfaction level:

  • Select: All star ratings.
  • 5 stars: Outstanding feedback.
  • 4 stars: Very positive feedback.
  • 3 stars: Moderate feedback with suggestions or neutral sentiment.
  • 2 stars: Below average feedback requiring seller attention.
  • 1 star: Critical feedback highlighting quality or fulfillment issues.

7. Description Keyword

Type specific keywords into the Description field to isolate reviews mentioning particular product attributes, such as "sizing", "battery", "fabric", or "delivery".

Applying and Closing the Drawer

  • Click the blue Apply Filters button at the bottom of the drawer to run your query and refresh the table.
  • To close the drawer without making changes, click the close button (×) in the top right corner or click anywhere outside the drawer.

Handling Empty Search and Filter Results

If you search for a term or apply filter criteria that do not match any existing reviews, the table presents a helpful empty state:

  • Notice: No reviews match your search or filters.
  • Quick Action: Click the Clear filters button to immediately reset all filters and return to your full product reviews list.

Pagination and Browsing Records

When your store accumulates more reviews than your selected display limit (for example, more than 10 reviews):

  • Page Numbers: Click numbers (1, 2, 3) to jump straight to that page of reviews.
  • Next Page (>): Advances one page forward.
  • Last Page (>|): Jumps directly to the final page in your reviews archive.
  • The active page is clearly highlighted in blue so you always know where you are.

Responsive & Mobile View

The Product Reviews workspace is fully responsive and designed to work smoothly on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers:

  • Mobile Navigation: Tap the hamburger menu in the top bar to open the navigation drawer and switch between dashboard sections.
  • Compact Table View: The table cleanly reorganizes columns on mobile devices, ensuring review text, buyer badges, and star ratings remain clear and readable.
  • Mobile Filter Drawer: The Apply Filters drawer smoothly slides over the screen, allowing you to filter feedback on the go.

Product Reviews vs. Seller Reviews Comparison

It is helpful to understand the distinction between the two review consoles available in your seller dashboard:

Feature / DetailProduct Reviews (/seller/seller-product-reviews/)Seller Reviews (/seller/seller-reviews/)
Review TargetSpecific individual products in your catalog.Your overall seller profile and store reputation.
Customer ScoringStandard 1 to 5 star rating.Multi-dimensional rating across Price, Value, and Quality.
Display LocationCustomer-facing Product Detail Page (PDP).Seller Profile page and Marketplace Seller List.
Verified BadgeShows Verified owner if the customer purchased the item.Indicates general customer review status.
Key BenefitHelps optimize individual product quality and descriptions.Builds overall store brand trust and seller credibility.

Best Practices for Managing Product Reviews

To make the most of customer feedback and grow your marketplace store:

  1. Regular Monitoring: Check your Product Reviews weekly to catch product flaws, sizing questions, or packaging issues before they affect more buyers.
  2. Prioritize 1-Star and 2-Star Feedback: Use the filter drawer to inspect low-rated reviews first. Look for recurring patterns—such as color discrepancies or unclear size guides—and update your product descriptions accordingly.
  3. Celebrate Bestsellers: Filter by 5 stars to identify your happiest customers and top-performing items. Consider featuring these products in your store promotions.
  4. Encourage Verified Buyers: Verified owner reviews hold substantial influence over prospective buyers. Encourage customers to share honest feedback after receiving their orders.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why is a customer's review showing as "Pending"?

A review displays the Pending badge when it is awaiting moderation by the marketplace administrator. Depending on store configuration, administrator approval may be required before reviews become visible on the public product page.

What does the "Verified owner" badge mean?

The Verified owner badge indicates that the review was written by an authenticated customer who purchased the product directly from your store.

Can I edit or delete a customer review directly?

Vendors cannot modify or delete customer reviews directly from their seller panel. If a review violates marketplace policy, contains offensive language, or is fraudulent, contact the marketplace administrator through the Ask to Admin screen to request a moderation review.

How do I view the public product page where the review appears?

In the Product / Date / Status column, click the product title link. The storefront product page will open in a new browser tab, showing the live customer reviews section.
