# Product Image Enhancer (Thumbnail AI Tool)

The **Product Image Enhancer** is an artificial intelligence tool engineered specifically for product thumbnails. It transforms ordinary photos into studio-grade e-commerce imagery while preserving the physical authenticity of the product.

| Raw Vendor Photo | AI-Enhanced Thumbnail |
|---|---|
| • Dim or uneven home lighting<br>• Distracting messy background<br>• Flat mobile camera angles | • Balanced professional studio lighting<br>• Crisp, pure white or styled scene<br>• High-converting lifestyle presentation |

---

## 1. Accessing the Image Enhancer

The Image Enhancer is located right where sellers manage their product visuals:

1. Log in to your **Seller Dashboard**.
2. Navigate to **Products** and open any existing product in **Edit** mode, or click **Add Product** to start a new listing.
3. Upload or select your base product photo in the standard **Product Image** box.
4. Directly beneath the uploaded image preview, click the dedicated trigger button:  
   **[ Enhance image with AI ]**

> **Location on Form:**  
> In the seller **Product Image** panel, directly below the main uploaded product thumbnail preview, vendors see the dedicated **[ Enhance image with AI ]** button.

::: tip Image Upload Required
You must have a base image uploaded in the thumbnail box before launching the enhancer. If the box is empty, upload your raw product photo first.
:::

## 2. Step-by-Step Enhancement Workflow

Clicking **Enhance image with AI** opens the dedicated side-by-side comparison modal:

| Left Column: Original Image | Right Column: AI Enhanced Preview |
|---|---|
| Displays the vendor's untouched starting photo for live visual comparison. | Displays the AI-generated high-resolution enhanced variation based on your prompt. |

### Step 1: Side-by-Side Visual Inspection
The modal opens with a clear dual-column layout:
- **Left Column (Original Image):** Displays your untouched base photograph so you can always reference your starting point.
- **Right Column (AI Enhanced Preview):** Displays the enhanced output once generated.

### Step 2: Formulate Your Enhancement Prompt
In the prompt field at the bottom of the modal, describe the environment, lighting, or staging you want:

| Desired Look | Recommended Prompt Formula | Example Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| **Clean Studio** | *"Place item on a seamless pure white studio background with soft commercial lighting and subtle natural contact shadows."* | *"Pure white background, clean product shot, soft diffuse lighting, studio quality."* |
| **Modern Lifestyle** | *"Place item naturally on a [surface] in a [setting] with soft ambient daylight."* | *"Place naturally on a rustic oak coffee table in a sunlit living room with subtle out-of-focus background greenery."* |
| **Luxury / Premium** | *"Set on a [luxurious surface] with dramatic accent lighting and elegant reflections."* | *"Set on a polished dark marble tabletop with warm side lighting and subtle depth of field."* |

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/marketplace-vendor-image-enhancer-modal-prompt.webp"><img src="./assets/marketplace-vendor-image-enhancer-modal-prompt.webp" alt="Side-by-Side Image Enhancer Modal with Prompt Input" /></a>

### Step 3: Generate the Enhancement
1. Click the primary **Generate** button.
2. The AI processes the image and returns a high-resolution preview in the right-hand panel in 10 to 20 seconds.
3. Review the result. If you want adjustments, tweak your prompt and click **↺ Regenerate**.

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/marketplace-vendor-image-enhancer-modal-result.webp"><img src="./assets/marketplace-vendor-image-enhancer-modal-result.webp" alt="Side-by-Side Dual Comparison Showing Original vs AI Enhanced Image Preview" /></a>

### Step 4: Apply to Product
1. When satisfied with the preview, click **Insert**.
2. Confirm the thumbnail replacement prompt.
3. The modal closes and automatically replaces the active image preview in your product form with the enhanced version, displaying the save reminder notice.
4. Click **Update Product** (or **Save Product**) on your dashboard to save the listing.

## 3. Key Differences: Image Enhancer vs. Content Generator

| Feature Dimension | Product Content Generator | Product Image Enhancer |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary Goal** | Drafts text copy (Titles, Summaries, Descriptions, Prices, Attributes). | Upgrades visual product photography and main thumbnail presentation. |
| **Where to Launch** | Top of Add/Edit form or "Create Product with AI" on Products list. | Button positioned directly below the product thumbnail box. |
| **Input Format** | Text brief + optional reference photos. | Uploaded original photo + text styling prompt. |
| **Output Type** | Form field values (text & numbers). | High-resolution optimized product photography. |
| **Application Mode** | Fills multiple form inputs across the page. | Swaps the featured product image attachment. |
