# Seller Panel Overview

The **Seller Panel** is where sellers and their team run their shop. It opens at `/seller/` on your store's address (for example, `https://your-domain.com/seller/vendor-dashboard/`), and you sign in on the **Seller Login** page.

## The Header

The header bar stays at the top of every page so you can quickly search, check notifications, and open your account settings:

- **Sidebar Toggle:** Click the menu icon at the top left to hide or show the sidebar. Hiding it gives you more room to view wide tables and charts.
- **Shop Logo & Name:** Shows your shop logo and store name (such as *Aurora Athletics*).
- **Mega Search:** Type to find your **Products**, **Orders**, and **Customers**:
  - Click any product or order in the results to open it right away.
  - Use **Explore all** to see the full list of matches.
  - Customer results cannot be clicked directly, so click **Explore all** to view them in your customer list.
- **Home Page:** Opens the store's main home page.
- **Visit Shop:** Opens your public shop page in a new tab so you can see how customers view your store.
- **Toggle Theme:** Switch between light and dark look. Your choice is saved in your browser.
- **Notifications Bell:** Shows alerts for new orders, low stock items, and messages from the admin.
- **Profile Menu:** 
  - **My Profile:** Opens your shop details, opening hours, policies, and KYC verification.
  - **Account Settings:** Change your password and account email.
  - **Logout:** Signs you out of your shop.

::: tip Subscription Countdown
When the store uses vendor subscription plans and your plan ends within nine days, the header also displays **"Subscription expires in: X days"** with a countdown. Click it to choose and renew your plan.
:::

## The Sidebar

The sidebar organizes all your daily tasks into clear menus:

| Menu | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Follow sales, view orders, check your profile score, and see what needs your attention. |
| **Store Edit** | Upload your shop logo and banner, set opening hours, and add your store policies. |
| **Catalog** | Add new products, view your items, upload products in bulk, and print barcode labels. |
| **Inventories** | Update stock counts inline, and set standard prices or scheduled sales. |
| **Sales** | View customer orders, schedule carrier deliveries, and verify Store Pickup codes. |
| **Performance** | Check your account health score and read customer reviews. |
| **Reporting** | See sales, product, and customer reports, and export them as CSV or PDF files. |
| **Customers** | See everyone who bought from you, and send updates to your shop followers. |
| **Communication** | Send messages to the store admin and read marketplace news. |
| **Vendor Staff** | Add team members and choose which menus they can use. |
| **Vacation** | Pause your shop and notify buyers when you take time off. |
| **Settings** | Update your password, pick product form layouts, and customize chart colors. |

---

### Collapse the Sidebar
Click **Collapse** at the bottom of the sidebar to show icons only. When collapsed, hover over any icon to see its sub-pages. This gives you maximum screen space for big tables.

---

### Top Navigation Tabs
When you open any menu (such as **Catalog** or **Inventories**), its pages also appear as tabs at the top of the screen. You can switch between them with one click without opening the sidebar again.

## Switch Between Dashboard Views (Classic View)

The marketplace gives you two ways to view your dashboard:

1. **Dedicated Seller Panel (`/seller/`):** The clean, full-screen workspace explained in this guide.
2. **Classic My Account View (`/my-account/`):** Displays your vendor tools inside the regular WooCommerce account page, matching your store's WordPress theme.

You can switch between them whenever you like:
- To switch to the theme view, click **Classic view** at the bottom of the sidebar.
- To return to the full-screen panel, click **Go to Seller Dashboard** inside your My Account menu.

## What Team Members See

If you have helpers or warehouse staff, you can add them under **Vendor Staff** (`/seller/vendor-staff/`):

- **Same Login Page:** Team members sign in on the same Seller Login page using their own username and password.
- **Allowed Menus Only:** They only see the menus you allow. For example, a packing assistant can see orders and shipping, but not your financial reports or earnings. Learn more in our [Vendor Staff Guide](https://wpdoc.webkul.com/woocommerce-marketplace/documentation/vendor-staff.html).
- **Owner-Only Sections:** Team members cannot open **My Profile**, request payouts, or see your profile score. Those settings remain private to the shop owner.

## Change Panel Language and Preferences

You can customize your seller tools under **Settings**:

- **Language & Password:** Go to **Settings > General** to change your login email or password. The panel uses the language set for your user account.
- **Product Form Layout:** Go to **Settings > Site Setting** to choose how you like to edit products:
  - *Horizontal Tabs:* Tabs across the top.
  - *Vertical Tabs:* A side menu on the left.
  - *Stacked Form:* All sections in one long scrollable page.
- **Graph Colors:** Go to **Settings > Graph Setting** to pick custom colors for your sales charts and performance graphs.
