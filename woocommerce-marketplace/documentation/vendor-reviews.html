Vendor Reviews & Ratings

In WooCommerce Multi-Vendor Marketplace, customer feedback plays a vital role in building buyer trust, increasing conversions, and growing your store reputation. The seller dashboard gives vendors dedicated consoles to track customer feedback across two distinct areas: Seller Reviews (overall store performance and service ratings) and Product Reviews (item-specific ratings and reviews).

Vendors can quickly search feedback by review title, customize list displays (5, 10, 20, 30, 40, or 50 items per page), sort records by newest or oldest, and use a slide-out filter drawer to inspect customer opinions by rating, moderation status, or date ranges.

Accessing Reviews in the Vendor Dashboard

Vendors can access their review pages from the side navigation menu of the seller panel:

  1. Log into your Seller Account.
  2. Locate the navigation sidebar and find the Performance group (or Engagement & Communication in your store setup).
  3. Select either:
    • Seller Reviews: View store-level feedback, overall ratings, and multi-dimensional scores across Price, Value, and Quality.
    • Product Reviews: View feedback and star ratings submitted for specific items in your catalog.
Review SectionDedicated Seller URLScope & Focus
Seller Reviews/seller/seller-reviews/Store service, fulfillment quality, seller communication, and dimensional ratings (Price, Value, Quality).
Product Reviews/seller/seller-product-reviews/Individual product performance, item quality, customer comments, and verified owner status.

Quick Access on Mobile

On smartphones or small screens, tap the Open menu button in the top bar to expand the sidebar drawer, open Performance, and tap Seller Reviews or Product Reviews.

Seller Reviews Management (/seller/seller-reviews/)

The Seller Reviews page displays all feedback submitted by customers directly regarding your store profile and seller performance.

Top Controls & Fast Search

At the top of the Seller Reviews table, you will find quick controls to find and organize reviews:

  • Search by review title: Enter keywords from the review headline (such as "Great maker", "Fast delivery", or "Quality") and press Enter to instantly filter results.
  • Items Per Page: Choose how many reviews to display at a time using the dropdown selector. Available options include:
    • 5
    • 10 (Default)
    • 20
    • 30
    • 40
    • 50
  • Sort By: Reorder your reviews list to match your analysis needs:
    • Newest first (Default): Brings the most recent reviews to the top.
    • Oldest first: Sorts reviews starting from your earliest feedback.
  • Filter: Click the Filter button to open the slide-out Apply Filters drawer.

Understanding the Seller Reviews Table

The table presents three comprehensive data columns designed to give you instant insight into buyer sentiment:

ColumnInformation DisplayedDescription
Customer / EmailCustomer Name & Email AddressDisplays the buyer's full name, registered email address, and profile avatar.
Status / DateStatus Badge & Submission DateShows the current review moderation status (Approved, Pending, or Disapproved) along with the calendar date when the review was submitted (e.g., August 10, 2026).
Title / Rating / DescriptionRating Breakdown & CommentsDisplays the aggregate score (e.g., Rated 5 out of 5), the multi-dimensional breakdown (Price, Value, and Quality scores from 1 to 5), the review headline title, and the full customer review message.

Using the Seller Reviews Filter Drawer

Clicking the Filter button opens the slide-out drawer on the right side of the screen. This allows you to combine multiple filters to isolate specific feedback:

  1. Customer: Type a customer's name to see feedback submitted by that specific buyer.
  2. Email: Enter an email address to filter reviews associated with a buyer account.
  3. Rating: Filter by star rating level:
    • Select (All ratings)
    • 5 stars
    • 4 stars
    • 3 stars
    • 2 stars
    • 1 star
  4. Description: Search for specific words or phrases mentioned inside customer comments.
  5. Status: Filter reviews by their moderation state:
    • Select (All statuses)
    • Pending (Under admin review)
    • Approved (Published on your public profile)
    • Disapproved (Declined by administrator)
  6. Date Range Presets: Choose a preset date interval with a single click:
    • Today
    • Yesterday
    • This Week
    • This Month
    • Last Month
    • Last 3 Months
    • Last 6 Months
    • This Year
  7. Custom Date Range: Use the From date and To date calendar pickers to inspect feedback received during an exact promotion period or holiday campaign.
  8. Apply Filters: Click the Apply Filters button to refresh the table. To close the drawer without saving, click the Close (×) icon.

Product Reviews Management (/seller/seller-product-reviews/)

The Product Reviews page allows sellers to track feedback left for specific products in their marketplace catalog. This helps vendors identify popular bestsellers, catch item quality issues early, and understand customer experiences with particular products.

Top Controls

  • Search reviews: Enter product names or review keywords into the search bar to locate reviews quickly.
  • Items Per Page: Select display density (5, 10, 20, 30, 40, or 50 reviews per page).
  • Sort By: Toggle between Newest first and Oldest first.
  • Filter: Opens the dedicated product review filter drawer.

Understanding the Product Reviews Table

ColumnInformation DisplayedDescription
Customer / EmailCustomer Details & Verified BadgeShows the buyer's name, email, and a Verified owner badge if the reviewer purchased the item from your store.
Product / Date / StatusProduct Info, Date & Approval StateDisplays the product title, submission date, and moderation state (Approved or Pending).
Rating / DescriptionStar Score & Product FeedbackDisplays the numerical star rating (e.g., Rated 4 out of 5) and the customer's written evaluation of the item.

Using the Product Reviews Filter Drawer

Click the Filter button to open the slide-out drawer and narrow down your product feedback:

  • Customer: Filter by customer name.
  • Email: Filter by customer email.
  • Date Presets: Quick selection (Today, Yesterday, This Week, This Month, Last Month, Last 3 Months, Last 6 Months, This Year).
  • Custom Date Range: Set specific From date and To date values.
  • Status: Filter by Approved or Pending reviews.
  • Rating: Select specific star levels from 5 stars down to 1 star.
  • Description: Filter by keywords within the product feedback text.
  • Apply Filters: Click to execute the search query.

Pagination & List Browsing

When review records exceed your chosen per-page threshold (e.g., more than 10 reviews):

  • Page Selectors: Click numbered buttons (1, 2, 3) to jump directly to any page.
  • Next & Previous: Use the > and < arrows to move forward or backward one page at a time.
  • First & Last Page: Click >| to jump directly to the final page of reviews.
  • Result Summary: The bottom bar displays the live record count, such as Showing 10 of 24 entries or Showing 3 of 3 entries.

Multi-Dimensional Rating Framework

When customers review your store profile on the marketplace storefront, they evaluate your business across three specific performance pillars:

                                         │

            ▼                            ▼                            ▼

  │   Price Rating    │        │   Value Rating    │        │  Quality Rating   │

  └─────────┬─────────┘        └─────────┬─────────┘        └─────────┬─────────┘
            │                            │                            │
            └────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────┘
                                         ▼
                          ┌────────────────────────────┐
                          │    Overall Store Rating    │
                          │   (Average of 3 Pillars)   │
                          └────────────────────────────┘
  1. Price (1 to 5 Stars): Reflects whether customers feel your product pricing is competitive, fair, and transparent.
  2. Value (1 to 5 Stars): Measures customer satisfaction regarding whether the item lived up to its price point and provided good return on investment.
  3. Quality (1 to 5 Stars): Evaluates material durability, packaging care, craftsmanship, and condition upon arrival.

The overall seller rating displayed on your profile is calculated as the average of these three pillars across all approved customer reviews.

To protect marketplace integrity, prevent malicious spam, and give prospective shoppers authentic buying confidence, all customer reviews pass through a controlled moderation lifecycle. Every review submitted across the platform is screened before publication on public vendor profiles and product pages.

Step-by-Step Lifecycle Guide

1. Customer Submission (Storefront)

  • Seller Reviews: Customers navigate to the vendor's public shop profile, select the Reviews tab, and complete the Write your review form by assigning 1 to 5 stars across Price, Value, and Quality, entering a review title, and describing their experience.
  • Product Reviews: Buyers submit item feedback on the product detail page with a star rating and written commentary. Verified purchasers automatically receive the Verified owner badge.

2. Automatic Quarantine in Moderation Queue

  • Immediately upon submission, feedback receives the Pending moderation status.
  • Pending feedback is strictly quarantined from public shop visitors to prevent abusive content, inappropriate links, or competitor smear attempts from surfacing on live pages.
  • Both Seller Reviews (/seller/seller-reviews/) and Product Reviews (/seller/seller-product-reviews/) display pending records in the vendor panel so sellers are aware of incoming reviews.

3. Administrative Moderation & Quality Screening

  • Marketplace administrators inspect queued entries in Marketplace → Reviews & Rating.
  • Administrators read the customer's full comments using the See More modal preview, verify order legitimacy, and verify compliance with content rules.
  • The administrator selects Approve or Disapprove individually or via bulk actions.

4. Storefront Publication & Real-Time Score Updates

  • Once an administrator marks feedback as Approved:
    • The review is published live on the vendor's storefront profile and/or product reviews tab.
    • The marketplace recalculates the vendor's overall rating: $$\text{Overall Store Rating} = \frac{\text{Average Price} + \text{Average Value} + \text{Average Quality}}{3}$$
  • If an administrator marks feedback as Disapproved, the entry remains hidden from public shoppers and does not impact seller star calculations.

5. Read-Only Integrity & Vendor Protections

  • Read-Only Access: Vendors can browse, search, and filter all customer feedback in their consoles, but cannot edit, modify, or delete reviews. This policy prevents biased rating manipulation and maintains customer trust.
  • Contesting Unfair Reviews: If an approved review contains false accusations, abusive language, or violates platform policies, vendors can contact the platform administrator using Ask to Admin to request re-moderation or deletion.

Best Practices for Maintaining High Seller Ratings

  • Ship Promptly: Timely dispatch and accurate tracking numbers lead to positive feedback and 5-star quality ratings.
  • Accurate Descriptions: Ensure your product titles, specifications, and images represent items truthfully to keep Value ratings high.
  • Careful Packaging: Safe, clean packaging protects items from damage during transit and leaves a great impression on buyers.
  • Inspect Low Ratings: Regularly filter your reviews table by 1 star and 2 stars to spot recurring customer concerns and address product or shipping defects.
  • Engage via Support: If a buyer leaves a question before reviewing, respond quickly using the Vendor Queries console.
