# Admin News – Vendor

The **Admin News** section allows vendors to stay updated with important announcements shared by the marketplace admin.

::: tip Administrator Guide
If you are a store administrator looking to create news posts, set expiration dates, or configure seller visibility, please refer to the [Admin News Management](https://wpdoc.webkul.com/woocommerce-marketplace/documentation/admin-news.html) documentation.
:::

---

## Access Path

Vendors can access marketplace announcements directly from their seller panel:

1. Log in to the marketplace with your vendor account credentials.
2. In the vendor dashboard left navigation menu, navigate to **News & Notifications → Admin News**.
3. You will arrive at the main **Admin News** feed page (`/seller/admin-news/`).

Breadcrumb navigation:
- **Dashboard → News & Notifications → Admin News**

---

## View Admin News

- Vendors can view all the news sent by the admin from their vendor dashboard.
- Only news assigned or made visible to vendors will appear, and vendors can click on any news item to read the full details.

### Admin News Feed Interface

The updated vendor panel presents announcements in an organized table with instant search capability.

#### Key Interface Elements

- **Search by News Title**: A dedicated search input located at the top right of the table. Enter any keyword or title term (for example, "Payouts" or "Policy") and press **Enter** to instantly filter matching news items.
- **Helper Notice**: A clear guideline reminding sellers: *"Click on the news title to read a news."*
- **Structured News Table**: Displays essential information at a glance:

| Column Name | Description | What Vendors See |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Title** | The headline of the announcement published by the admin. | A clickable link that opens the complete announcement article. |
| **Description** | A brief preview or summary excerpt of the news post. | Gives vendors a quick overview before opening the full article. Ends with an ellipsis (`...`). |
| **Published On** | The date when the administrator published the announcement. | Formatted clearly by month, day, and year (e.g., *October 3, 2026*). |

---

## View News Details

- By tapping on any news item, the vendor can view the complete description and details of that news.
- This helps vendors stay informed about marketplace updates, policies, or announcements.

### News Detail Screen Elements

When clicking any news headline from the table, a dedicated article card opens with the following details:

- **Back to News List Button (`←`)**: Located beside the headline. Clicking this arrow icon returns you directly to the Admin News table without losing your position.
- **Announcement Headline**: Displayed prominently at the top of the reading card.
- **Full Article Content**: Displays the complete text provided by the marketplace administration, including:
  - Detailed policy explanations and terms.
  - Important dates, schedules, or cut-off times.
  - Bulleted steps and operational instructions.
  - Hyperlinks to relevant dashboard sections (such as Payouts, Transactions, or Profile Settings).

## Step-by-Step Vendor Workflow

Following this standard process ensures you never miss critical marketplace updates:

1. **Check the Admin News Section Regularly**: When logging into your seller dashboard, check the **Admin News** section under **News & Notifications** for new announcements.
2. **Scan Headlines and Dates**: Review the **Published On** column and recent headlines to identify urgent notices or seasonal guidelines.
3. **Use the Title Search**: If you are looking for a specific topic (such as payout dates, holiday hours, or commission updates), type the keyword into the **Search by news title** field.
4. **Read the Full Announcement**: Click on the headline link to view the entire content.
5. **Return to News Feed**: Click the **Back (`←`)** button next to the title or use the breadcrumbs to return to the news list.

## Types of Announcements Shared by Admin

Marketplace administrators use Admin News to communicate essential business updates, such as:

- **Payout & Commission Schedules**: Information regarding payout dates, processing cycles, and withdrawal cutoff hours.
- **Policy & Agreement Updates**: Revisions to marketplace seller terms, return policies, or vendor service level agreements (SLAs).
- **Platform Features & Tools**: Announcements regarding new seller dashboard features, such as product comparison, vacation mode, or shipping integrations.
- **Sales Campaigns & Promotions**: Invitations to join marketplace-wide seasonal sales (e.g., Autumn Sale, Black Friday, or Holiday fairs).
- **Scheduled Maintenance Alerts**: Advance notifications regarding technical maintenance windows or platform updates.
