Seller Quick Start

This guide follows a new vendor through the complete journey from initial registration to receiving your first order payout on the WooCommerce Multi-Vendor Marketplace. Each step provides practical instructions and links directly to the detailed configuration and operational documentation.

Seller Onboarding ChecklistPre-launch check

Before publishing your first live product, ensure you have configured your Shipping Zones in the seller panel and added your store branding in Profile Settings. Stores with complete profiles and transparent shipping terms convert shoppers significantly faster.

Phase 01 • Account Setup & Branding
01Merchant Sign-Up

1. Create Your Seller Account

Open the marketplace registration page or the dedicated Seller Central onboarding page on the marketplace site:

  • Check the "Become a Seller" checkbox during customer registration, or navigate directly to the merchant sign-up form.
  • Fill in your basic user details: full name, email address, password, store name, and choose a unique shop URL slug (e.g., your-store-name for yourdomain.com/shop/your-store-name).
  • Read and accept the marketplace vendor terms and conditions before submitting your registration.
  • Detailed Guide: See Vendor Registration Process and Configuration Seller Central.
02Account Approval

2. Wait for Approval and Sign In

Depending on marketplace platform policies:

  • Auto-Approval: If automatic vendor approval is active, you are immediately logged in to your new seller dashboard.
  • Admin Review: If manual admin moderation is enabled, your account will be placed under review. Once reviewed and approved by platform administrators, you will receive an automated confirmation email containing your login credentials and dashboard access link.
  • Detailed Guide: See Vendor Registration Process and Email Configurations.
03Profile & KYC

3. Complete Your Shop Profile & KYC

Building a recognizable brand identity is essential for converting store visitors into loyal customers:

  • Store Identity: Upload your high-resolution store banner, company avatar/logo, store name, and an engaging store description.
  • Contact & Address: Enter your official business address, contact telephone number, and customer support email.
  • Social Profiles: Connect your brand's social media accounts (Facebook, Twitter/X, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube) to display follow icons on your shop header.
  • KYC Verification: If required by the platform, submit your business registration, tax identification number, or government photo ID to unlock product publishing and payout privileges.
  • Payment & Payout Details: Configure your preferred withdrawal payout method (PayPal email address or direct bank transfer account details) so earnings can be disbursed promptly.
  • Detailed Guides: See Vendor Profile and Vendor KYC.
04Command Center

4. Explore the Vendor Dashboard

Sign in to your dedicated seller panel to view your centralized marketplace command center:

  • Live Analytics: Monitor real-time gross revenue, net vendor earnings, total orders, and published product counts.
  • Order Overview: Quickly see pending orders, processing shipments, and completed deliveries.
  • Recent Inquiries & Alerts: View customer queries and important platform announcements broadcast by the marketplace administrator.
  • Detailed Guide: See Vendor Dashboard Guide.
Phase 02 • Shipping & Product Catalog
05Shipping Logistics

5. Set Up Your Shipping Zones & Methods

Before listing physical merchandise, configure the shipping methods and geographical areas you service:

  • Shipping Zones: Add custom zones based on specific countries, states, or postal code groups.
  • Shipping Methods: Enable and price Flat Rate, Free Shipping, or Local Store Pickup.
  • Shipping Classes & Calculations: Assign rates per item, per shipping class, or based on minimum order subtotal requirements.
  • Store Pickup with OTP: Allow local customers to collect their purchases in person with secure one-time passcode verification.
  • Detailed Guide: See Vendor Shipping Zone Management.
06Catalog Inventory

6. Add Your Products to the Catalog

Populate your storefront with merchandise across all supported WooCommerce product types:

  • Simple Products: Standard physical products with defined regular prices, sale prices, inventory levels, and SKUs.
  • Variable Products: Complex items offering customer choices across sizes, colors, and styles, with variation-specific pricing and stock tracking.
  • Virtual & Downloadable Products: Digital eBooks, software downloads, access keys, or consulting services without physical shipping requirements.
  • Select & Sell (Existing Products): Join existing catalog listings already present on the marketplace to offer your items at your own price and stock without creating duplicate product listings.
  • AI Product Content (Magic AI): Use built-in Generative AI prompts to generate engaging product titles, long descriptions, and feature bullet points with a single click.
  • Detailed Guides: See Vendor Product Management and Product Settings.
07Storefront Go-Live

7. Product Review and Storefront Publishing

Ensure your product listings adhere to marketplace catalog standards:

  • Review Workflow: If product approval is enabled by the platform, submitted items show a Pending Review status until approved by an administrator. Once reviewed, they are published directly to the public catalog.
  • Immediate Publishing: If auto-approval is active, your products appear immediately on your shop page and in marketplace search results.
  • Detailed Guides: See Vendor Product Management and Vendor Management & Product Assignment.
Phase 03 • Sales, Fulfillment & Growth
08Marketing & Reach

8. Promote Your Shop and Build Followers

Drive targeted traffic to your personalized store page:

  • Public Shop URL: Share your dedicated shop storefront (yourdomain.com/shop/your-vendor-slug) on your marketing channels and social media.
  • Store Followers: Shoppers can follow your shop by clicking the "Favorite Shop" or "Follow Shop" button to receive automated email alerts when you publish new catalog items.
  • Coupons & Special Offers: Create promotional discount codes or volume deals to incentivize buyers during seasonal events.
  • Detailed Guides: See Vendor Shop Followers and Vendor Profile.
09Order Fulfillment

9. Receive and Fulfill Orders

When shoppers purchase items from multiple sellers in a single checkout, the platform automatically splits the cart into individual sub-orders for each participating merchant:

  • Order Notifications: Receive instant email alerts and dashboard notifications the moment an order is placed.
  • Order Management: View the buyer's shipping address, item details, customer notes, and payment status.
  • Fulfillment & Tracking: Update order status to Processing or Completed, assign shipping carriers, and provide package tracking numbers so customers can follow their delivery.
  • Invoices & Packing Slips: Download or print branded PDF vendor invoices and packing slips to include inside the shipment.
  • Detailed Guides: See Vendor Order Management and Vendor Invoice.
10Wallet & Payouts

10. Track Earnings and Request Payouts

Keep total visibility over your finances and receive your earnings reliably:

  • Commission Transparency: The marketplace automatically deducts its agreed platform commission and credits the remainder to your vendor wallet.
  • Transaction History: Review a clear ledger of credited sales, commission fees, and completed payout disbursements.
  • Withdrawal Requests: When your balance reaches the marketplace's minimum withdrawal threshold, submit a payout request directly from your dashboard to receive funds via PayPal, Stripe, or Bank Transfer.
  • Detailed Guides: See Vendor Payout Management and Vendor Transaction Management.
11Store Reputation

11. Maintain High Performance, Reviews & Support

Grow a thriving, sustainable business on the marketplace:

  • Customer Feedback & Ratings: Encourage happy buyers to leave reviews on your shop and product pages. Maintain high rating averages to boost your storefront placement. See Vendor Reviews & Ratings.
  • Customer Pre-Sale Queries: Respond promptly to customer questions submitted through the product page query tabs. See Vendor Queries Management.
  • Ask Admin for Support: Reach out to the platform administration team directly through the built-in query system for assistance with policies, commissions, or account questions. See Ask to Admin.
  • Staff Accounts: Add team members (staff accounts) with custom role permissions to help manage inventory, customer inquiries, and order shipping. See Vendor Staff.
  • Vacation Mode: Going away or temporarily unable to fulfill orders? Enable vacation mode to pause checkout or post a temporary notice banner without losing your products or ratings. See Vendor Vacation.
