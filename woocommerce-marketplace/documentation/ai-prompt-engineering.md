# Prompt Customization & Best Practices

Crafting clear, specific prompts helps **Magic AI** generate polished, accurate, and high-converting product listings. This guide provides recommended prompt structures, best practices, and copy templates for both store administrators and marketplace vendors.

::: tip Golden Rule of AI Prompts
The more specific and descriptive your starting input (materials, dimensions, target audience, brand tone), the more complete and accurate the generated product listing will be.
:::

---

## The Anatomy of an Effective Product Prompt

An ideal prompt covers four core elements:

| Prompt Component | Key Information to Include | Practical Example |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Core Product** | Product type, model, and form factor | *"AeroTune Pro wireless over-ear noise-cancelling headphones"* |
| **2. Materials & Specs** | Dimensions, weight, technical specifications | *"40mm titanium drivers, Bluetooth 5.4, 45-hour battery life"* |
| **3. Target Audience** | Intended buyer persona and primary use cases | *"Remote professionals, frequent travelers, and daily commuters"* |
| **4. Tone & Pricing** | Desired copy style and target pricing | *"Modern, premium, professional tone. Target price: $149"* |

## High-Converting Copy Templates

### 1. Electronics & Gadgets
> **Prompt Example:**  
> *"Wireless over-ear noise-cancelling headphones, 40mm titanium drivers, Bluetooth 5.4, 45-hour battery life with USB-C fast charging (5 mins = 4 hours). Memory foam ear cushions, foldable travel design, built-in dual beamforming microphones for calls. Target audience: remote professionals and commuters. Target price: $149."*

**What Magic AI Produces:**
- **Product Name:** *AeroTune Pro Wireless Noise-Cancelling Over-Ear Headphones (45H Battery, Bluetooth 5.4)*
- **Short Description:** *Block out distractions and immerse yourself in premium acoustic sound with 40mm titanium drivers, active noise cancellation, and a plush memory-foam travel design.*
- **Long Description:** Comprehensive breakdown of acoustic engineering, battery longevity benchmarks, call clarity, and in-the-box contents.

---

### 2. Apparel, Fashion & Accessories
> **Prompt Example:**  
> *"Women's oversized cable-knit turtleneck sweater, 100% organic merino wool, heavyweight 7-gauge knit, ribbed cuffs and hem, drop shoulders. Available in Oat Milk and Charcoal. Gentle hand wash only. Designed for cozy autumn and winter layering. Target price: $88."*

**What Magic AI Produces:**
- **Product Name:** *Merino Wool Oversized Cable-Knit Turtleneck Sweater*
- **Short Description:** *Stay effortlessly warm with pure organic merino wool, featuring an artisan cable-knit texture and relaxed drop-shoulder silhouette for chic cold-weather styling.*
- **Long Description:** Fabric provenance details, styling tips (pairs with denim or skirts), sizing recommendations, and wool garment care instructions.

---

### 3. Home, Furniture & Kitchenware
> **Prompt Example:**  
> *"Handcrafted ceramic pour-over coffee dripper with matching 500ml glass carafe. Matte speckled ivory glaze on stoneware, heat-resistant borosilicate glass, silicone base ring. Fits standard V-shaped size 02 paper filters. Dishwasher safe. Target price: $42."*

**What Magic AI Produces:**
- **Product Name:** *Artisan Stoneware Pour-Over Coffee Maker Set with 500ml Borosilicate Carafe*
- **Short Description:** *Brew cafe-quality pour-over coffee at home with handcrafted ceramic heat retention and an ergonomic heat-safe serving carafe.*
- **Long Description:** Step-by-step brewing guide, extraction benefits of stoneware thermal mass, material safety specifications, and cleaning tips.

## Multimodal Vision Prompts (Using Reference Photos)

When uploading reference photos of physical items, combine your image with a focused prompt:

- **State What the Photo Shows:** *"See attached 2 photos of our handcrafted leather messenger bag. Highlight the antique brass hardware, double-stitched corners, and laptop compartment shown in photo 2."*
- **Clarify Hidden Features:** *"The photo shows the exterior matte black finish; please note that the interior features waterproof nylon lining with 3 slip pockets."*

## Image Enhancer Prompt Templates

When using the **Vendor Image Enhancer** below the product thumbnail, use these proven styling prompts:

### 1. Clean Studio & Pure White (Amazon & Marketplace Standard)
> *"Remove the cluttered room background. Place the product on a seamless pure white studio surface with soft, diffused commercial lighting and subtle, natural contact shadows underneath."*

### 2. Modern Lifestyle & Warm Interior
> *"Keep the product completely unchanged, but place it naturally on a natural oak dining table in a sunlit Scandinavian apartment with gentle ambient daylight and blurred background greenery."*

### 3. Luxury / Moody Boutique
> *"Place the item on a dark reflective black granite surface with dramatic rim lighting, soft reflections, and subtle golden bokeh in the deep background."*

### 4. Outdoor / Adventure
> *"Place the hiking boot on a rugged granite mountain ledge with warm sunrise sunlight casting long soft shadows."*

## Prompt Engineering Do's & Don'ts

| Do's | Don'ts |
|---|---|
| **Do:** Include numerical specs (battery hours, dimensions, capacity). | **Don't:** Avoid vague single-word prompts like "shoe" or "phone". |
| **Do:** Specify your target customer (students, athletes, luxury buyers). | **Don't:** Don't ask the AI to guess technical standards without guidance. |
| **Do:** Mention care and warranty details for richer descriptions. | **Don't:** Avoid conflicting instructions (e.g., "cheap but luxury"). |
| **Do:** Use single-field inline buttons to tweak specific sections quickly. | **Don't:** Don't republish without reviewing pricing and stock numbers. |
