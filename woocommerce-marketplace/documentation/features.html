Features

The Webkul WooCommerce Multi Vendor Marketplace plugin offers a robust, scalable, and enterprise-ready feature set that empowers both marketplace owners and vendors. It is designed to handle high traffic, multiple sellers, and complex order workflows with ease.

Core Marketplace Features

1. Multi-Vendor Registration

Allow unlimited vendors to register on your platform. Vendors can sign up using the default WooCommerce registration form or a dedicated Seller Central onboarding page. This helps you easily onboard new sellers without manual intervention.

2. Separate Vendor Profile Pages

Each vendor gets a dedicated shop page containing:

  • Shop logo
  • Banner image
  • About section
  • Product collection
  • Reviews & ratings

This helps vendors build their own brand identity within your marketplace.

3. Magic AI

Supports multiple AI providers:

  • OpenAI
  • Anthropic
  • Gemini
  • xAI
  • Groq
  • DeepSeek
  • Mistral
  • Ollama
  • WordPress AI Connectors

Vendors can:

  • Create products using AI-powered content generation
  • Enhance product images with AI

Admins can:

  • Set product generation limits for individual vendors
  • Generate product and category images and product descriptions using AI

4. Vendor Shipping Management

Vendors can configure their own shipping:

  • Create shipping zones
  • Add shipping methods
  • Define shipping costs
  • Manage shipping classes

This allows vendors to control logistics independently.

5. Store Pickup

Customers can:

  • Select store pickup as a shipping method
  • Verify their pickup using an OTP sent to their email address.

Vendors and admins can:

  • Manage and track store pickup orders

Vendors can:

  • Set store hours for pickup

Admins can:

  • Configure Store Pickup settings

Order emails include:

  • Pickup-related information and details

6. Schedule Pickup

Admins can:

  • Configure Fulfilment Carriers for Free Shipping and Flat Rate Shipping orders

Vendors can:

  • Select an available shipping provider or use their own
  • Share shipment and tracking details with customers
  • Ship an order with an admin-listed carrier or Self Ship
  • View the carrier, items, and tracking number in the Shipments section of the order view

7. Featured Products

Admins can mark specific vendor products as featured to:

  • Increase product visibility
  • Boost sales
  • Highlight trending products

8. Admin-like Vendor Dashboard

Admins can enable an advanced dashboard view for vendors that resembles the WordPress admin panel. Vendors can switch between:

  • Frontend dashboard (simple)
  • Admin-style dashboard (advanced)

This provides flexibility for both beginner and power sellers.

9. New Seller Panel

Vendors can:

  • Access a new seller panel with a grouped sidebar menu for Catalog, Sales, Inventories, Reporting, Performance, and Settings
  • Use the mobile menu on small screens
  • Switch to the Classic view at any time
  • Open the panel from the Go to Seller Dashboard button in My Account

10. Vendor Query System

Vendors can submit queries to admins directly from their dashboard. Admins can:

  • Reply to queries
  • Track conversations
  • Maintain a history of communication

This ensures smooth communication and support.

11. Vendor Profile Management

Vendors can manage:

  • Personal details
  • Shop information
  • Social media links
  • Return, shipping & privacy policies

This improves transparency and customer trust.

12. Vendor Staff Management

Vendors can:

  • Create and manage staff members for their stores
  • Assign and manage staff permissions

Staff members can:

  • Access only the features granted through their assigned permissions

Admins can:

  • Manage the vendor staff permissions list.
  • View every vendor staff user in one list
  • Edit staff names and permissions
  • Delete staff members individually or in bulk

13. Advanced Product Management

Vendors can create multiple product types:

  • Simple
  • Grouped
  • External/Affiliate
  • Variable
  • Downloadable

They can manage:

  • Pricing
  • Inventory
  • Images
  • Attributes
  • Variations

Vendors can also:

  • Create products in a 3-step flow with product type cards
  • Track a Listing Completeness score with links to each missing field
  • Edit the product permalink
  • Add a GTIN, UPC, EAN, or ISBN to products and variations

Admins also retain control to edit or remove any product.

14. Bulk Upload

Vendors can:

  • Import and update products in bulk using CSV files
  • Add and update Select & Sell offers through CSV upload
  • Download ready-made CSV templates for products and offers
  • Update existing products and offers by matching SKU
  • Export products to CSV, edit them, and re-upload
  • Map columns and validate every row before import
  • View imported, updated, skipped, and failed counts after each import
  • Download a report that explains why each row failed or was skipped
  • Receive an email when admin approves or rejects newly imported products

Admins can:

  • Configure Bulk Upload settings under Marketplace > Settings

15. Select & Sell

Vendors can:

  • Sell admin-created simple and variable products through their own stores
  • Search the marketplace catalog to find a product and sell it
  • Set their own price, stock, description, and status for each offer
  • Manage offers from dedicated Select & Sell screens in the vendor panel

Customers can:

  • View the seller list and store selector on product pages
  • Choose their preferred vendor

Admins can:

  • Configure Select & Sell settings under Marketplace > Settings
  • Approve or reject Select & Sell offers from the Product Approval queue

16. Order Management

Vendors can:

  • View order details
  • Access billing & shipping info
  • Update order status
  • Print invoices
  • Upload custom invoices
  • Set the number of orders shown per page
  • Request payment for completed orders

This ensures professional order handling.

17. Vendor Approval System

Admins have full control over:

  • Approving new vendors
  • Approving vendor products
  • Blocking suspicious sellers
  • Approving or rejecting vendor products and Select & Sell offers from the Product Approval queue
  • Adding a reason with the decision

This protects marketplace quality.

18. Product Assignment & Transfer

Admins can:

  • Assign products to vendors
  • Transfer products between vendors
  • Reassign ownership if required

Useful for vendor migration and management.

19. SEO-Friendly URLs

Vendor profiles and product pages use clean URLs, improving:

  • Google ranking
  • Click-through rate
  • Search visibility

20. Flexible Commission System

Admins can configure:

  • Global commission
  • Vendor-specific commission
  • Fixed commission
  • Percentage commission
  • Hybrid (fixed + percentage)

This allows complete revenue control.

21. Favorite Vendors & Shop Followers

Customers can:

  • Follow vendors
  • Receive updates
  • Get notified about new products

This increases repeat sales.

22. Bulk Product Assignment

Admins can:

  • Assign multiple products to vendors in bulk
  • Save time on large catalogs

23. Upsell & Cross-Sell Products

Vendors can:

  • Suggest premium alternatives
  • Promote related products
  • Increase average order value

24. WordPress Multisite Support

Fully compatible with:

  • Multisite networks
  • Centralized management
  • Multiple stores

25. HPOS Compatibility

Optimized for:

  • WooCommerce High-Performance Order Storage
  • Faster order processing
  • Large-scale stores

26. WooCommerce Blocks Support

Compatible with:

  • Cart block
  • Checkout block
  • Latest WooCommerce UI

Advanced Marketplace Features

27. Vendor Transactions

Admins can:

  • Transfer vendor earnings
  • Track payouts

Vendors can:

  • View transaction history
  • Monitor earnings
  • View earning summary cards on the Transactions & Earnings screen
  • Request payment, subject to the admin setting and withdrawal threshold

28. Vendor-wise Category Control

Admins can:

  • Assign specific categories to vendors
  • Control what they can sell

29. Global Product Type Control

Admins can:

  • Enable/disable product types
  • Maintain marketplace standards

30. Pricing Management

Vendors can:

  • Manage product and variation pricing from a separate section
  • Update regular prices, sale prices, and sale schedules from a single screen
  • Select multiple products and apply bulk price updates
  • Apply bulk updates to the regular price, sale price, or both prices
  • Set a price, or use Increase By and Decrease By options
  • Enter the bulk value as a fixed amount or a percentage
  • Filter products from a filter drawer

31. Inventory Management

Vendors can:

  • Manage product and variation inventory from a separate section
  • Update stock, SKU, GTIN, and other inventory details in bulk
  • Use Increase By and Decrease By options to update stock in bulk
  • Manage sold individually, maximum purchasable quantity, and stock management settings
  • Filter products from a filter drawer

32. Barcode Management

Vendors can:

  • Generate barcodes for products and variations
  • Choose from Code 128, EAN-13, and UPC-A formats
  • Print barcodes in PDF format
  • Generate and print barcodes in bulk
  • View each variation's barcode on the Product Barcodes page
  • Control barcode access through staff permissions

33. Vendor Review Moderation

Admins can:

  • Approve reviews
  • Remove fake reviews
  • Maintain platform trust

34. Email Template Management

Supports:

  • Default WooCommerce emails
  • Marketplace-specific emails
  • Custom branding

35. Custom Dashboard Endpoints

Admins can customize:

  • Dashboard URLs
  • Menu labels
  • Navigation structure

36. Refund Management

Vendors can:

  • Initiate refunds
  • Restock products
  • Track refunded amounts

37. Product Approval Notifications

Admins get real-time alerts when:

  • Vendors add products
  • Vendors edit products

38. Google Analytics Integration

Admins can:

  • Track sales
  • Monitor product performance
  • Analyze customer behavior

39. Seller Dashboard

Vendors can:

  • View sales, orders, and customers charts with a date range filter
  • Track order status, recent orders, and Top 5 Selling Products
  • Use the Action Required card for pending orders, orders to ship, low stock products, and notifications
  • Monitor Total Payout, Remaining Payout, Customer Feedback, Account Health, Trends, and Profile Score

40. Reports

Provides reports on:

  • Sales
  • Products
  • Customers

Reports include:

  • Sales
  • Commission
  • Orders
  • Tax & Tax Rates
  • Refunds
  • Payment methods
  • Top products
  • Top customers

Vendors can:

  • View and analyze reports specific to their stores
  • Download reports in CSV and PDF formats

41. Customers

Vendors can:

  • View customer details, total revenue, order count, and last order date
  • Filter customers by name, email, registered and guest accounts

42. Seller Performance

Vendors can monitor:

  • Account health
  • Seller reviews
  • Product reviews

Account Health:

  • View an overall health score out of 100 with Healthy, At Risk, and Unhealthy bands
  • Track order cancellation rate, customer feedback, and flags on the store and products
  • See more insights on refund rate, product reviews, and paid to completed time
  • Choose a custom period to review performance
  • Learn how the score is calculated from a built-in guide
  • Test changes to the score with a live calculator

Seller Reviews:

  • View store reviews with price, value, and quality ratings
  • Check the status of each review as Pending, Approved, or Disapproved
  • Search by review title and sort by newest first
  • Filter by customer, email, rating, description, status, and date

Product Reviews:

  • View reviews on their products with the Verified owner label
  • Search, sort, and filter by customer, email, date, status, rating and description

43. WPML Multilingual Support

Supports:

  • Multi-language marketplaces
  • Vendor product translation
  • Global audience

44. Google Map Integration

Displays:

  • Top billing countries

45. Enhanced Vendor Profiles
