# Customer List

In **WooCommerce Multi-Vendor Marketplace**, building strong relationships with your buyers is key to growing your online business. The **Customer List** provides vendors with a dedicated directory of every customer who has purchased products from their store.

Instead of navigating through individual order invoices, vendors can view all buyers in one organized table. The interface displays buyer names, email addresses, total store revenue generated, completed order counts, account types (registered or guest), and the date of their most recent purchase.

## Why the Customer List is Useful for Vendors

Keeping track of your customer base helps you make informed business decisions:

- **Identify Repeat Buyers:** Spot loyal shoppers who place multiple orders with your shop.
- **Track Customer Value:** View total revenue generated by each customer to recognize high-value buyers.
- **Distinguish Account Types:** Differentiate between registered marketplace members and guest shoppers who completed checkout without an account.
- **Monitor Purchase Recency:** See the date of each buyer's latest purchase to understand customer retention.
- **Quick Communication:** Easily find customer contact email addresses for order follow-ups or customer support.

---

## How to Access the Customer List

Vendors can access their Customer List directly from the Seller Dashboard sidebar navigation:

1. Log in to your **Seller Account**.
2. In the left navigation menu, locate the **Customers** section.
3. Click **Customers** to open the buyer directory.

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/vendor-customers-sidebar-nav.png"><img src="./assets/vendor-customers-sidebar-nav.png" alt="Locating Customers in the Seller Sidebar Navigation" /></a>

| Navigation Item | Location / Path | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Menu Group** | **Customers** | Dedicated navigation section containing *Customers* and *Shop Follower*. |
| **Menu Link** | `Customers` &rarr; `Customers` | Opens the main customer directory table. |
| **Direct URL** | `/seller/seller-customers/` | Direct browser address to access the Customer List screen. |

::: tip Mobile Navigation
If you are managing your store on a smartphone or tablet, tap the **Open menu** hamburger button in the top navigation bar, expand the **Customers** section, and select **Customers**.
:::

## Understanding the Customer Directory Table

The Customer List presents four structured columns designed to give vendors immediate clarity regarding buyer identity, purchasing habits, and account standing.

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/vendor-customers-list-overview.png"><img src="./assets/vendor-customers-list-overview.png" alt="Customer Directory Table Columns and Rows" /></a>

### 1. Customer
The first column identifies the buyer:
- **Initials Avatar:** A colored circular badge containing the customer's initials (for example, **DC** for *Demo Customer* or **AS** for *Amelia Silva*) for rapid visual identification.
- **Full Name:** The buyer's name entered during checkout or saved in their registered account.
- **Email Address:** The customer's contact email displayed right beneath the name for convenient reference.

### 2. Revenue / Orders
This column highlights the commercial value of the customer to your shop:
- **Total Revenue:** The total gross dollar amount spent by the buyer on your products (for example, `$558.80` or `$199.76`).
- **Total Orders:** The cumulative count of completed or processing orders containing items from your store (for example, `6 orders`, `2 orders`, or `1 order`).

### 3. Group
The Group column indicates the customer's account registration status:
- **Registered:** The customer possesses an active registered account on the marketplace platform.
- **Guest:** The buyer checked out using guest checkout mode without creating a persistent store account.

### 4. Last Order
Displays the calendar date when the customer placed their most recent order with your shop (for example, *November 19, 2026* or *October 14, 2026*). This enables vendors to see which buyers have purchased recently versus those who may require re-engagement.

## Quick Search and Toolbar Controls

The top toolbar offers straightforward controls to search, filter, and adjust the number of displayed records:

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/vendor-customers-list-overview.png"><img src="./assets/vendor-customers-list-overview.png" alt="Customer List Toolbar Controls" /></a>

### Results Counter
On the left side of the toolbar, the counter dynamically displays how many buyers match your current view (for example, `14 Results` by default, or `1 Result` when filtered).

### Search Bar
Vendors can quickly find specific buyers using the search input field:
1. Type a customer's name (for example, *Demo*) or partial email address into the **Search** box.
2. Press **Enter** on your keyboard.
3. The table instantly refreshes to show only matching customer profiles.

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/vendor-customers-search-result.png"><img src="./assets/vendor-customers-search-result.png" alt="Customer List Instant Search Results" /></a>

### Items Per Page Selector
Customize how many rows appear on each page. Click the dropdown to switch between:
- `5`
- `10` *(Default)*
- `20`
- `30`
- `40`
- `50`

Selecting a value automatically updates the table and recalculates pagination.

### Filter Button
Click the **Filter** button to open the slide-out filter panel for more specific search parameters.

## Using the Filter Drawer

For targeted buyer analysis, the slide-out **Filter Drawer** enables vendors to refine customer lists using multiple criteria simultaneously.

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/vendor-customers-filter-drawer.png"><img src="./assets/vendor-customers-filter-drawer.png" alt="Slide-out Filter Drawer with Customer, Email, and Group Fields" /></a>

### Filter Options

1. **Customer:** Enter a full or partial customer name (for example, *Amelia*) to filter records by specific buyer identity.
2. **Email:** Enter a full or partial email address (for example, *example.test* or a specific domain) to isolate accounts.
3. **Group:** Filter buyers based on account creation status using the dropdown:
   - **Select:** Default view showing both registered and guest buyers.
   - **Registered:** Restricts results exclusively to registered account holders.
   - **Guest:** Restricts results exclusively to guest checkout customers.

### How to Apply and Reset Filters

1. Click the **Filter** button in the top toolbar to reveal the drawer.
2. Fill in the desired fields or choose an account **Group**.
3. Click the **Apply Filters** button at the bottom of the drawer.
4. The drawer closes, and the table updates with matching records.

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/vendor-customers-filter-registered.png"><img src="./assets/vendor-customers-filter-registered.png" alt="Filtered Customer List Showing Registered Buyers with Active Reset Option" /></a>

::: tip Clearing Filters
When a filter is active, a convenient **Reset** button appears next to the **Filter** button in the top toolbar. Click **Reset** at any time to clear your search criteria and return to the complete customer list.
:::

## Pagination and Navigation

When your customer count exceeds the selected per-page limit, pagination links appear beneath the table:

- **Showing entries label:** Displays your current view range (for example, *Showing 10 of 14 entries*).
- **Page Numbers:** Click numbers (such as `1` or `2`) to jump directly to that page.
- **Navigation Arrows:** Use `>` to proceed to the next page, or `>|` to jump directly to the last page of records.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the difference between a Registered buyer and a Guest buyer?
A **Registered** buyer has created an account on the marketplace website, allowing them to log in, track past orders, and manage saved addresses. A **Guest** buyer completed checkout using guest checkout mode without creating a login password. Both groups appear in your Customer List whenever they purchase your products.

### Does the Customer List show customers who bought from other sellers?
No. The Customer List in your Seller Dashboard is strictly isolated to your own storefront. You will only see buyers who have purchased items belonging to your catalog.

### Can I search for customers by partial email addresses?
Yes. You can use either the top toolbar search input or the Email filter inside the drawer to search by partial names, email usernames, or email domains (such as `@gmail.com` or `@company.com`).

### How is the Revenue figure calculated for each customer?
The revenue total represents the cumulative dollar amount of all purchases the customer has made with your shop across processing and completed orders.
