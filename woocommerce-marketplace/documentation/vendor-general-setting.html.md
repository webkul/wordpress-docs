# General Setting

The **General Setting** screen allows marketplace vendors to manage their personal user account credentials, login email address, profile avatar, and account password within the dedicated seller panel.

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/seller-general-settings-overview.png"><img src="./assets/seller-general-settings-overview.png" alt="Vendor General Setting Overview in Seller Panel" /></a>

::: tip Account Settings vs. Store Edit
It is important to understand the difference between **General Setting** and **Store Edit**:
- **Settings > General (`/seller/edit-account/`)**: Controls your **personal user account** (your login email, account password, personal name, and profile picture).
- **Store Edit (`/seller/vendor-profile/`)**: Controls your **public storefront** (your shop logo, store banner, shop description, business address, store hours, payment payout methods, and return policies).
:::

---

## Accessing General Settings

To open your personal account settings in the seller panel:

1. Sign in to your **Vendor Account** on the marketplace.
2. From the left sidebar navigation, locate the **Settings** menu.
3. Click **General** from the submenu.
4. You will arrive at the **General Setting** page (`/seller/edit-account/`).

You can also track your exact location using the breadcrumb trail displayed at the top of the workspace:  
`Dashboard > Settings > General`

## Managing Your Account Details

The account details form lets you keep your personal contact information accurate and up to date.

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/seller-general-settings-form.png"><img src="./assets/seller-general-settings-form.png" alt="Vendor Edit Account Form" /></a>

### 1. Profile Avatar Image

At the top of the form, you can personalize your merchant profile with an avatar picture:

- **Add an Avatar**: If no photo is set, click the circular **Add Image** icon (with the camera symbol) to select an image from your computer.
- **Update Existing Photo**: Hover over your current profile picture and click the **Delete (Bin)** icon to remove it, then click **Add Image** to upload a replacement photo.
- **Accepted Formats**: Standard web image formats (`JPG`, `PNG`, `WebP`).
- **Recommended Dimensions**: A square image (for example, `256 × 256 px` or `512 × 512 px`) ensures your photo displays cleanly across circular badges and headers.

### 2. Account Information Fields

The account form contains the following required fields:

| Field | Required | Description | Best Practice |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **First name** | Yes (`*`) | Your given first name. | Use your legal or business primary contact name. |
| **Last name** | Yes (`*`) | Your family surname. | Ensures clear identity verification on customer receipts and administrative records. |
| **Display name** | Yes (`*`) | How your name appears publicly in account headers, review responses, and comments. | Choose a recognizable name for buyers and marketplace staff. |
| **Email address** | Yes (`*`) | The primary email address linked to your user account. | Used for signing in, receiving order alerts, low stock notices, and password reset links. Ensure this inbox is monitored daily. |

::: warning Updating Your Email Address
Changing your email address updates your primary login username for future sign-ins. Make sure you enter a valid, active email address so you do not miss critical customer inquiries or order updates.
:::

## Updating Your Password

You can change your password at any time directly from the **Password change** section located on the same page.

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/seller-general-settings-password.webp"><img src="./assets/seller-general-settings-password.webp" alt="Password Change Section" /></a>

### Step-by-Step Password Update:

1. Scroll down to the **Password change** fieldset.
2. Enter your credentials into the three password fields:
   - **Current password (leave blank to leave unchanged)**: Type your existing password. *(Required only when setting a new password).*
   - **New password (leave blank to leave unchanged)**: Type your desired new password.
   - **Confirm new password**: Re-type the exact same new password to verify there are no typographical errors.
3. Click the **Save changes** button at the bottom of the form.

::: tip Password Security Best Practices
To keep your store secure from unauthorized access:
- Use at least **10 to 12 characters**.
- Combine uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols (such as `@`, `#`, `$`, `%`).
- Avoid using common phrases, your shop name, or predictable personal dates.
- If you do not wish to update your password when changing your name or email, simply leave all three password fields empty.
:::

---

## Saving Changes & Confirmation

Once you have filled out your updated information:

1. Click the primary **Save changes** button at the bottom left of the form.
2. The page reloads and displays a green confirmation message at the top:
   > *"Account details changed successfully."*
3. If any required information is missing (such as an empty First name or an invalid email format), the browser alerts you immediately to correct the field before saving.

## Global Search & Header Navigation

While on the **General Setting** screen, you also have access to the seller panel's top navigation utilities:

- **Quick Search Bar (`Search Orders/Products`)**: Located in the upper header bar. You can type any product title, SKU, or order ID and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to jump straight to filtered results in your product catalog or order history without navigating away.
- **Sidebar Navigation**: Easily switch to other settings categories such as **Graph Setting** (for customizing chart colors) or **Site Setting** (for choosing product form navigation styles).
- **Classic View Switcher**: Click the **Classic view** button at the bottom of the left sidebar if you prefer using the traditional WooCommerce My Account layout instead of the full-screen panel.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Does changing my display name here change my public shop name?
No. Your public shop name and brand identity are configured under **Store Edit** (`/seller/vendor-profile/`). The display name in General Settings only applies to your personal user profile, review replies, and account navigation.

### What should I do if I forget my current password?
If you cannot remember your current password to complete the change form, log out of your account, click the **Lost your password?** link on the login screen, and follow the email reset link sent to your registered email address.

### Can staff members edit my General Settings?
Only users with granted permissions can access account settings. By default, vendor team sub-accounts cannot access sensitive owner credentials. For details on customizing team capabilities, see the [Vendor Staff Guide](https://wpdoc.webkul.com/woocommerce-marketplace/documentation/vendor-staff.html).
