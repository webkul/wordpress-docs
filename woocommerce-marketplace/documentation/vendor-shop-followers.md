# Vendor Shop Followers

The **Vendor Shop Followers** feature allows vendors to engage directly with buyers who follow their shop by sending notifications and updates.

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/vendor-shop-followers-overview.webp"><img src="./assets/vendor-shop-followers-overview.webp" alt="Vendor Shop Followers Management Overview" /></a>

Building a loyal customer base is essential for marketplace success. With this feature, buyers can follow their favorite marketplace stores with a single click, while vendors gain a direct communication channel to broadcast new arrivals, seasonal promotions, and shop announcements.

---

## Shop Followers Overview

- Buyers can follow a vendor’s shop directly from the **product page**.
- A buyer becomes a shop follower by clicking the **heart-shaped icon** on the product page.

### How Buyers Follow a Store

To follow a vendor's shop, buyers interact with the product page:

1. **Log into Customer Account**: The buyer must be logged in to an active customer account. The follow icon is hidden for guest visitors and the vendor themselves.
2. **Locate the Heart Icon**: On any single product page, the heart-shaped icon appears right next to the vendor's store link under the product details.
3. **Click to Follow**:
   - Clicking the heart icon adds the vendor to the customer's favorite stores list.
   - The heart icon immediately highlights to indicate the followed status.
4. **Click to Unfollow**: Clicking the highlighted heart icon again removes the vendor from the buyer's favorite list.

## Buyer Experience: Managing Favorite Vendors

Buyers can easily keep track of all the shops they follow through their customer account portal:

1. Navigate to **My Account** on the storefront.
2. Click on the **My Favorite Vendors** menu item.
3. In this section, buyers can:
   - View all stores they are currently following.
   - Click any vendor store name to visit their shop page directly.
   - Remove vendors from their favorites by checking the box and clicking the delete button.

## Manage Shop Followers

- Vendors can view all their shop followers under the **Shop Followers** section in the vendor dashboard.
- This section displays the complete list of followers associated with the vendor’s shop.

### Accessing the Shop Followers Dashboard

Vendors can manage their follower community directly from the seller console:

1. Log into the **Seller Dashboard**.
2. From the sidebar navigation, select **Shop Followers** (accessible at `/seller/vendor-shop-followers/`).
3. The page presents a dedicated management table with quick actions, real-time search, and sorting controls.

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/vendor-shop-followers-dashboard.webp"><img src="./assets/vendor-shop-followers-dashboard.webp" alt="Vendor Shop Followers Dashboard" /></a>

---

### Top Action Controls

Above the follower table, vendors have two primary action buttons:

- **Delete Follower (Trash Icon)**: Allows vendors to permanently remove one or more selected followers from their list in bulk.
- **Send Notification (Bell Icon)**: Opens the email broadcast composer to send updates to all selected followers.

---

### Search and Sort Followers

Vendors with large customer followings can quickly find and organize their follower list using the built-in toolbar:

- **Search Followers**: Enter a customer name or email address in the search box to find specific followers instantly.
- **Sort By**: Reorder the followers list using the dropdown menu:
  - **Newest first** *(Default)*: Shows the most recent followers at the top.
  - **Oldest first**: Displays the earliest followers first.
  - **Name A-Z**: Arranges followers alphabetically by customer name.

---

### Understanding the Followers Table

The followers table organizes key follower information into clear columns:

| Column | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Select Checkbox** | Use the header checkbox to select all followers on the page at once, or use individual row checkboxes for targeted actions. |
| **Customer Name** | Displays an avatar circle with initials and the customer's full registered name. |
| **Customer Email** | Shows the follower's registered email address. |
| **Action** | Provides a direct **Remove** link to delete a single follower from your shop's list. |

::: tip Follower Count & Pagination
Below the table, vendors can see their total follower statistics (e.g., *Showing 1 of 1 entries*) along with pagination controls when followers span across multiple pages.
:::

## Send Notifications to Followers

- Vendors can send notifications to their shop followers to:
  - Share updates.
  - Promote new products.
  - Inform about offers or announcements.
- To send a notification:
  - Select one or more followers from the list.
  - Click on **Send Notification**.

### Selection Requirement

Before clicking **Send Notification**, the vendor must select at least one follower using the table checkboxes:

- **Single Follower**: Check the box next to any specific follower row.
- **Multiple Followers**: Check the boxes for multiple selected followers.
- **All Followers**: Click the top checkbox in the table header to select all followers currently displayed.

::: note Selection Validation
If you click **Send Notification** without checking any follower box, an alert prompt will appear: *"Please select some data to proceed."*
:::

## Notification Popup

- A popup window appears to enter the notification details.
- After entering the required information:
  - Click **Send Mail** to dispatch the notification email to the selected followers.

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/vendor-shop-followers-notification-modal.webp"><img src="./assets/vendor-shop-followers-notification-modal.webp" alt="Send Notification Popup Modal" /></a>

### Modal Fields and Features

The **Send Notification** popup includes the following elements to help you compose effective emails:

| Element | Type | Purpose & Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Header & Close** | Title & Button | Displays the modal heading **Send Notification** and a close icon (`×`) to cancel at any time. |
| **Introductory Text** | Guidance | Explains: *"Email the followers you selected. They will receive it from your store."* |
| **Recipient Counter** | Dynamic Counter | Displays the exact audience size (e.g., *"Sending to 1 selected follower(s)"*), so you always know who will receive the email. |
| **Subject** | Text Input *(Required)* | Enter the email subject line (e.g., *"Exclusive Weekend Sale: 20% Off All Items"*). If left empty, an error warning alerts *"Subject field cannot be empty."* |
| **Message** | Textarea *(Required)* | Enter your announcement, discount code, product launch details, or personalized message. If left empty, an error warning alerts *"Message field cannot be empty."* |
| **Send Mail** | Action Button | Dispatches the email notification directly to all chosen followers from your vendor store. |

After sending, a confirmation message alerts that the email was dispatched successfully.

## Vendor Staff Permissions

If you use the **Vendor Staff** management feature to delegate store operations to team members, you can assign granular permissions for the Shop Followers section:

- **View followers**: Permits staff members to view the shop followers table, search customers, and sort entries.
- **Remove followers**: Allows staff members to delete followers individually or via bulk actions.
- **Send notification to followers**: Grants permission to open the notification modal and broadcast emails to shop followers.
